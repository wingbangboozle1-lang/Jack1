After-tax module (K-4)

One after-tax number from verified rules

Turns the verified federal, California and LA transfer-tax rules into one after-tax net sale figure, so it can be set against the hold case. Only the owner and CPA can supply the inputs; the page supplies none.

Private browser calculator · blank by design

After-tax net sale proceeds

Every input starts blank. Nothing is sent, stored or cached: no network request, no cookie, no local storage. Reloading the page clears it. Enter figures from the CPA, the draft purchase agreement and the title company, not estimates. Leave a field blank if you do not have a verified figure; the result then shows what is missing.

Shown only if a hold-case value is entered above. The two numbers must be on the same basis.

Limits

What this module does not compute

  • Short-term gain (held one year or less): taxed at ordinary rates, which need the full bracket table. The calculator stops rather than guessing.
  • Federal stacking is simplified: the long-term gain is layered above taxable income plus the unrecaptured section 1250 amount; the CPA confirms on the Schedule D worksheet (flag flag_4c386ad4_ltcg_stacking).
  • The 1031 like-kind exchange path (deferral, replacement identification and receipt windows) is a separate decision and is not netted here.
  • Losses, installment sales, §1245 recapture, AMT, state conformity differences, and the owner's other deductions are not modeled.
  • Rates and thresholds are the values in the table above. Federal 2026 amounts apply to a 2026 sale; California is the 2025 schedule (flags flag_4c386ad4_fed_year and flag_4c386ad4_ca_schedule_year). Sale-year figures must be re-read before use.
  • Who pays transfer tax and the ULA reading are open (flags flag_4c386ad4_transfer_tax_payer and flag_4c386ad4_ula_band_reading).
  • The output is one input to the sell-versus-renew choice, not the choice. Do not treat it as a filing figure.
