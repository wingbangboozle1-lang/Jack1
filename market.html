Market & area

The area is not growing. The question is whether the corner is.

Central Los Angeles is not one market. Census, SCAG and the Department of Finance each publish at a different geography, and none of them publishes at the scale of a restaurant trade area. The figures below describe the city and the county. They establish the direction of travel; they do not describe the corner this box stands on.

Read the geographies before reading the numbers City of Los Angeles, Los Angeles County, the LA–Long Beach–Santa Ana metro area, and the LA–Long Beach–Anaheim CPI area are four different boundaries. This site labels every row with its geography. Two unemployment rates appear in the tables below and they are not comparable: 4.4% is the metro area in September 2026, 5.8% is the city's 2025 annual average excluding October, and 4.5% is the county's September 2026 reading.
