Why the numbers do not agree

The 4.31% asking cap is an absolute-NNN corporate lease with roughly 15 years of term. The 5.82% figure is a ground lease with roughly 13 years of term and a much smaller price point. Different structure, different tenant obligation, different risk — so a single 'market cap rate' for this asset does not exist. That is why the decision framework does not hinge on one cap rate: it hinges on lease term, which you control by negotiating a renewal.
