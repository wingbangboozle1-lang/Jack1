Manual review checklist

  • Confirm the exact lease expiration date, option language and notice deadlines from the executed lease.
  • Confirm the legal tenant and any guaranty; do not assume the public issuer is the obligor.
  • Run the parcel lookups: ZIMAS, Assessor portal, Tax Rate Area, FEMA flood, CAL FIRE FHSZ, CGS seismic zones.
  • Re-check the indexed ULA thresholds and rates in force on the actual closing date.
  • Monitor the issuer's EDGAR filings index for subsequent quarterly results and closure guidance.
  • Verify insurance declarations, retrofit status and a current appraisal before any sale pricing.

Official lookup tools: ZIMAS · Assessor portal · Tax Rate Area · FEMA flood maps · CAL FIRE FHSZ · CGS seismic zones · EDGAR filings.
