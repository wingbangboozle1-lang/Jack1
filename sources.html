Evidence snapshot · 08 Nov 2026

Source register · method · limitations

Every table row has an official review link.

Sources are linked directly to official agencies, regulators, public-company filings, or public lookup tools. A link to a lookup tool does not mean a parcel query has been completed or that the linked source verifies the subject property.

How to read the evidence

Verified figures below describe the named geography, period, and source. Unverified property values remain empty and are marked Flagged for Review. No owner or property identifiers are published. The website is an analytical aid, not an appraisal, legal opinion, engineering report, insurance quote, or tax recommendation.

Method and data handling

Verification rules

How facts are presented

  • Retain the source’s official geography, period, units, and caveats.
  • Keep City of Los Angeles, Los Angeles County, SCAG region, and Downtown Plan metrics separate.
  • Corporate SEC figures are labeled issuer-wide, not unit-, tenant-, or guarantor-specific.
  • Show unknown property-specific cells blank and name the evidence needed to verify them.
  • Do not infer parcel identity, school assignment, hazard exposure, fair value, or sale / renewal outcome.

Important technical caveats

  • SEC financial statements present the amounts in thousands of U.S. dollars; the site retains that scale for exact comparisons.
  • The 2026 SEC 10-Q compares 12-week and 40-week periods and presents Del Taco as discontinued operations after its sale.
  • City unemployment (BLS 2025) excludes October; the county figure is a separate, not-seasonally-adjusted December series from the BLS LAUS county table.
  • SCAG and Downtown planning values are forecasts or planning expectations, not guaranteed asset-level outcomes.
  • Current Measure ULA thresholds can change annually and may not be applicable outside City of Los Angeles.
