Method

How this brief was built

  1. Blank means unverified. No value cell is ever filled with an estimate, an average used as a proxy, or a plausible-looking number. If it could not be read from the official page, the cell is empty and the item is flagged.
  2. Every data row carries its own link. The build step refuses to emit a table row without an official-source hyperlink, so a claim can always be traced to its publisher in one click.
  3. Geographies are named, never blended. City, county, metro, SCAG region, and Downtown plan area are all different shapes. Each row states which one it is.
  4. Measurement basis is stated. Household-survey unemployment and establishment-based employment are not the same thing, and the site does not average them together.
  5. Privacy by construction. No owner name, address, APN, ownership entity, lease document, or personal financial detail is published, requested, or stored. An automated check rejects visible email addresses and phone-number patterns on every page.
  6. Corrections are recorded, not erased. Figures that earlier drafts published and that could not be confirmed are listed on Flagged for Review with the reason, rather than quietly deleted.
Privacy-first: no personal information is collected, embedded, or published. Property identifiers are intentionally omitted.
