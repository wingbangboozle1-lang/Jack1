Rules

The verification rules this site is built on

  1. One row, one sourced metric. No row aggregates several figures or blends periods.
  2. Verified means read. The value was displayed on the cited official page during this session and is reproduced in verification/captures/.
  3. Derived means arithmetic, and the formula is shown. Derived rows list both operands and the operation in the note. 4 rows are derived.
  4. Unverified means blank. If an official source does not publish the value, the cell shows an em dash. No estimate, no interpolation, no third-party aggregator, no "approximately". 33 rows are blank on this basis and are listed on Flagged for review.
  5. Withheld means private. Owner, address, parcel, rent, loan and basis data are never published. 10 rows are withheld.
  6. Conflicts are published, not resolved by preference. Where two official sources differ, both appear and the difference is named.
  7. Assumptions are labelled. The scenario model runs on seven named assumptions (A-01 to A-08) on the model page. No assumption is presented as data.
What this site is not It is not an appraisal, a broker opinion of value, a tax opinion, or a personal financial recommendation. It does not know the owner's basis, debt, liquidity needs, estate plan or risk tolerance, and it does not ask. Those private factors can override every conclusion here.
