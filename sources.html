Sources, methodology and verification log

The rule is simple and absolute: a number appears only if it was read on an official page during this session. Otherwise the cell stays empty and the row is flagged.

Verification rules

Status taxonomy

StatusMeaningRequirement
verifiedValue read on the cited official page in this session. Source must be a government, central-bank or SEC publication; URL must resolve to that page.
derivedArithmetic performed on two verified values. Both inputs cited; formula stated in the note.

Method, in order

Frame the geography before the claim. No parcel identifier was supplied, so the analytical unit is Los Angeles city and Los Angeles County. Every sub-geography claim is either sourced at that level or left blank.
Collect only from primary publishers. Census, BLS, California Department of Finance, California DIR, CGS, CAL FIRE/OSFM, California Department of Insurance, LA County Assessor, City of Los Angeles, NCES, SEC EDGAR, and the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. No aggregator, no blog, no "market report" and no press summary was used as a source of record.
Transcribe, do not transform. Values are copied exactly as published, including vintage labels (e.g. "V2025", "September 2026 (preliminary)"). Unit conversions are labelled as derived.
Separate fact from assumption by construction. The projection model takes verified anchors (Treasury yield, ULA rate, CPI) and explicit assumptions (cap rates, renewal probability). The two are never mixed in the same column, and every assumption is listed with its reason.
Publish the gaps. Every blank is listed on Flagged for Review with the official link that would close it.

Automated checks run on this build

What the build script verifies

node scripts/verify-data.mjs asserts, for all 130 records: (1) every row has a unique ID and a category; (2) every non-blank value has a status of verified or derived; (3) every unverified row has an empty value — the check fails the build if a number sneaks into a flagged row; (4) every source URL is https and on the official-domain allowlist; (5) every internal link in every page resolves to a file that exists; (6) no page references a missing asset. The current build passes all six.

What it cannot verify, stated honestly

The build environment has no general outbound internet access, so the script cannot perform live HTTP checks on the external source links. Those links were instead opened and read individually during research, and the excerpts are kept in research/notes/. The automated pass protects the structure of the data; the manual pass protects the values. Both are documented.

Glossary

NNN (triple net)

A lease in which the tenant pays base rent plus property taxes, insurance and maintenance. It protects the owner's income from expense inflation; it does not protect against vacancy or tenant default.

Cap rate

Net operating income divided by price. Lower cap = higher value. It embeds the tenant's credit, the remaining lease term and the cost of capital.

Measure ULA

A City of Los Angeles transfer tax of 4.0% of the full price above $5m and 5.5% above $10m, paid by the seller.

Prop 13 / Prop 8

California caps assessed-value growth at 2% a year (Prop 13) and allows a temporary reduction when market value falls below the assessed value (Prop 8).

Dark value

What the property is worth if the unit is empty — land plus a building looking for a new use. It is the floor under the hold decision.

Alquist-Priolo zone

A mapped regulatory zone around an active fault trace where new occupied structures must be set back (generally 50 feet) and disclosed at sale.
