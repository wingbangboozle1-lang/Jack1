Source registry & verification protocol

Every figure on this site traces to one of the sources below. Official = primary government/filing, accessed 2026-11-06. Secondary = reputable republisher (re-verify at primary). Market = broker data (no official exists). Nothing is on this site without a source or an explicit flag.

Verification protocol (how this site was built)

  1. Collection. Data pulled 2026-11-06 from official portals (census.gov, bls.gov, bea.gov, fred.stlouisfed.org, cde.ca.gov, lausd.org, sec.gov/EDGAR, fire.ca.gov, lacounty.gov, lacity.gov/CAO, metro.net, lahsa.org, la28.org, dir.ca.gov, boe.ca.gov, usgs.gov). Browser-transcribed into the registry with access date.
  2. No hallucinations. If a number could not be confirmed on an official page, it was either omitted (left blank) or published on Flagged for Review with the reason. Mixed-measure confusions (current vs real GDP; output loss vs total loss) were kept separate and labeled.
  3. Link-per-row. Every table row on every page carries its own source link(s) and a status badge; the master list below deduplicates them.
  4. Privacy. No owner, occupant, store number, address, APN, or lease economics are published. Location described only at district level. This repo contains no personal information.
  5. Currency & staleness. Census V2025 population estimates are July 2025; ACS income is 2020–2024; SAIPE 2024; BEA county GDP 2024; BLS MSA unemployment 2025 (Dec-2025 print 4.8%); 10Y Treasury Oct-2026 = 5.13%; fire figures as of the County's anniversary fact sheet. Stale items (e.g., Mar-2024 cap comps) are marked stale.
  6. Re-verification before action. Any transaction decision must re-pull live figures (caps, 10Y, ULA threshold, lease estoppel) at the moment of LOI/escrow. Prices and taxes move; this site is a snapshot.
