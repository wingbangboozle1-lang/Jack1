Projections

A deterministic, reproducible projection system for the trade area and the asset — with every assumption printed on the page.

What this model is and is not

Model output. Not an appraisal. Not a forecast of any official agency. All inputs are either official statistics (cited) or explicit analyst assumptions listed below.

How the model is built

  1. It loads official series from data/series.json (CPI, food-away-from-home CPI, unemployment, employment).
  2. It applies the explicit analyst assumptions printed below. None are hidden.
  3. Value is computed as rent ÷ cap rate, expressed as a multiple of today's value, because the actual rent is unverified (Flagged F-01/F-02).
  4. Running python3 tools/model.py regenerates every number on this page.

How to read it

  • value_relative_* is a multiple of today's market value, not dollars.
  • value_relative_renew > value_relative_hold at every year because the renewal both removes residual decay and re-rates the cap rate.
  • value_relative_stress_no_renewal shows what the same asset is worth if rates drift 20 bp/yr instead of 5 bp/yr while you wait.
  • None of these are guarantees; they are the arithmetic consequence of the assumptions printed above.

The honest limitation

These are not forecasts of an official agency and they are not an appraisal. They are the arithmetic consequence of the printed assumptions, which is exactly what makes them auditable. Change an assumption, re-run, and see what changes.
