Privacy & Method

Anonymized by design: placeholders instead of a parcel, its owner, its tenant, and its rent.

Privacy was the first requirement of this project, so it shaped the data model, not just the wording.

Why this is the stricter reading, on purpose

California already restricts publishing address-linked records: State Board of Equalization Letter To Assessors No. 2025/010 (implementing AB 1785) amended Government Code §7928.205 to bar state and local agencies from publicly posting, on the internet, an official's home address, telephone number, or both the name and assessor parcel number associated with it. This project applies the stricter version of that rule to every person, not only officials.

How verification and privacy reinforce each other

Because the parcel is withheld, any figure that would require looking up the parcel (assessed value, FHSZ zone, seismic zone) is left blank and flagged rather than estimated. Privacy therefore removes the temptation to invent a number.

What this site does technically

  • Static HTML only. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no fonts loaded from third parties.
  • External links are marked rel="noopener".
  • The site is generated from data/*.json; nothing is typed by hand into a page.

Privacy: this site contains no street address, parcel number, owner identity, tenant contact or lease financials. Parcels are shown as [PARCEL-REDACTED].

Verification: every factual cell links to its official source. Empty cells are unverified and are listed on Flagged for Review. Data retrieved 2026-11-06.
