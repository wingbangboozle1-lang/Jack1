Tax, Regulatory, Insurance & Transaction-Cost Environment

For a decision to sell, transaction taxes and Proposition 13 reassessment are first-order. For a decision to hold, the tax cap and the tenant’s NNN reimbursement obligation are first-order.

Measure ULA is a cliff, not a bracket. It charges 4% or 5.5% of the ENTIRE consideration or value, only inside the City of Los Angeles, on top of county and city documentary transfer tax. Whether the parcel is inside the City is therefore a binary, order-of-magnitude swing in net sale proceeds — and it is currently unknown.
Why it matters. For a decision to SELL, transaction taxes and Proposition 13 reassessment are first-order. For a decision to HOLD, the property-tax cap and the tenant's NNN obligation to reimburse taxes are first-order. Both sides of the decision are governed by the same body of law, so this page is load-bearing rather than background.

Verification standard. Every table row on this site carries a link to an official publisher — a government agency, a regulator, a central bank, a regional planning agency, or a document filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the value cell is left blank, the row is marked Unverified, and the reason is registered on the Flagged for Review page. No value on this site is inferred, estimated from memory, or taken from a news article, brokerage, aggregator or wiki.

Privacy. The subject property is deliberately anonymised. Its address, parcel number, owner identity, purchase price, rent, net operating income and insurance details are withheld and are not published anywhere on this site, including in the underlying data files. Every parcel-specific and owner-specific field is therefore blank by design and flagged as such.
