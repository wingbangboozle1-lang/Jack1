Rules that change net proceeds and tenant margins

Measure ULA is a cliff: the tier applies to the whole consideration once a threshold is crossed, and thresholds are indexed annually. Proposition 13 limits assessed-value growth while a property is held and reassesses on a change of ownership. The California fast-food wage floor applies to franchisees as well as brand owners.
