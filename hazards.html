Black swans are documented, not hypothetical

The USGS puts the 30-year probability of a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in the Los Angeles region at 60%. The County of Los Angeles documents the 2025 Eaton and Palisades fires with 16,251 structures destroyed, roughly 6,800 businesses affected and $5.2–$10.1 billion of lost economic output. The California Department of Insurance approved a $1 billion FAIR Plan assessment. All of these are regional figures: no parcel-level hazard rating is asserted.

The tail risk is correlation

A tenant credit event and a regional catastrophe are not independent for a single undiversified asset. Insurance, retrofit status and concentration limits are therefore part of the decision rule, not an afterthought.
