What the numbers say together

Tight, not growing

4.4% unemployment is full employment. But total nonfarm employment is up 0.5% year over year, and the city has lost 0.8% of its population since 2020 while the county lost 3.2%. A quick-service restaurant here competes for share in a market that is not expanding — which is exactly why the tenant's own same-store sales are negative.

The wage floor is the constraint

California's fast-food minimum wage is $20.00/hr, statutorily above the $16.90 state rate, and the Fast Food Council can raise it again. The state rate itself escalates with CPI-W, capped at 3.5% a year, and cannot be cut. The tenant's largest controllable cost has an upward ratchet written into law.

Deep demand, thin growth

$81.35 billion of retail sales and $17.37 billion of food-service sales is a very large pool. Household income of $81,939 against 16.5% poverty describes a market that is polarised — consistent with a value-oriented QSR, which is the brand's positioning.

What is missing at this granularity

Retail asking rents, sub-market vacancy, trade-area traffic counts and the parcel's own zoning cannot be sourced from an official statistical series. No broker report is substituted for them. They are published as blank and flagged on the Flagged for Review page.
