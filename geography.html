Geography is the constraint on every other number

"Central Los Angeles" is not an official statistical geography. The published geographies here are the City of Los Angeles, Los Angeles County and the Los Angeles–Long Beach–Anaheim MSA. Parcel-specific zoning, transit catchment, school assignment and hazard designation require an address and are blank.
