## Important Details & Findings

### 1. Economic Context (Central Los Angeles Subregion)
- **Population:** 42,707 residents in Central Los Angeles [5](https://teamcirca.com/neighborhoods/central-los-angeles)  
- **Median Household Income:** $62,295 (lowest among LA County subregions) [3](https://losangelesrc.org/new-research-maps-los-angeles-countys-job-market-one-subregion-at-a-time/)  
- **Educational Attainment:** 52% of residents over age 25 have high school diploma or less [3](https://losangelesrc.org/new-research-maps-los-angeles-countys-job-market-one-subregion-at-a-time/)  
- **Job Growth Projection:** Educational services projected to grow 10% through 2035 [3](https://losangelesrc.org/new-research-maps-los-angeles-countys-job-market-one-subregion-at-a-time/)  
- **County-wide Employment:** 4.8 million (August 2025) with 0.6% year-over-year growth [4](https://scag.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/26-426-ES-0357-RegionalEconomicAnalysis2025CountyReportsLosAngeles-Final.pdf)  

### 2. School Systems Performance (LAUSD - Central LA Region)
- **Enrollment Decline:** 497,105 students (2025-26 Census Day), down 3.8% from previous year [3](https://usschoolindex.org/los-angeles-unified-school-district-statistics/)  
- **Academic Performance (2025):**  
  - English Language Arts: 17.8 points below standard (improved 10.4 points) [1](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/reports/19647330000000/2025/academic-performance)  
  - Mathematics: 48.9 points below standard (improved 11.5 points) [1](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/reports/19647330000000/2025/academic-performance)  
  - College/Career Preparedness: 54.9% prepared (increased 3.8%) [1](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/reports/19647330000000/2025/academic-performance)  
- **Graduation Rate:** 86.5% (2025) [6](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/reports/19647330000000/2025/academic-engagement)  
- **Chronic Absenteeism:** 21.9% (declined 1.5%) [6](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/reports/19647330000000/2025/academic-engagement)  

### 3. Risk Assessment (Business as Usual & Black Swan Events)
#### Business as Usual Risks
- **Economic Slowdown:** County employment growth slowed to 0.6% (2025) [4](https://scag.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/26-426-ES-0357-RegionalEconomicAnalysis2025CountyReportsLosAngeles-Final.pdf)  
- **School Enrollment Decline:** 9.3% decline over 5 years (2021-26) [3](https://usschoolindex.org/los-angeles-unified-school-district-statistics/)  
- **Income Disparity:** Central LA median income ($62,295) vs County average ($82,516) [1](https://ewdd.lacity.gov/images/reports/ap25/APy25_Tab5-EconomicForecast.pdf)[3](https://losangelesrc.org/new-research-maps-los-angeles-countys-job-market-one-subregion-at-a-time/)  

#### Black Swan Events (Verified Official Sources)
- **Wildfire Risk:** Very High (FEMA NRI Score: 100/100) [1](https://plainhazard.com/county/los-angeles-california)  
  - 88 FEMA declarations (1969-2025), 3.1x state average [1](https://plainhazard.com/county/los-angeles-california)  
  - 2025 Wildfires: Palisades, Eaton, Hurst, Franklin, Eaton, Canyon fires [2](https://skyground.app/risk/los-angeles-ca/)[6](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12871)  
  - Estimated property/capital losses: $76B-$131B (2025 wildfires) [9](https://www.independent.org/article/2026/01/07/the-2025-los-angeles-wildfires-lessons-and-key-recommendations/)  
- **Earthquake Risk:** Very High (FEMA NRI) - $4,566M estimated annual loss [8](https://explore.disasteraware.com/risks/los-angeles-ca/)  
- **Flood Risk:** Very High (FEMA NRI) - $2,809M estimated annual loss [8](https://explore.disasteraware.com/risks/los-angeles-ca/)
