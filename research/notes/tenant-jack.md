## Analyst-relevant reading (NOT a sourced fact — labelled as assessment on the site)
System store count is shrinking (2,191 -> 2,136 -> guided 2,050-2,100), franchise same-store sales are negative, the dividend is gone, and management is explicitly monetising real estate. For a landlord whose rent depends on a single unit, the probability that the tenant renews at market rent at expiry should not be underwritten as high.

## Irregularity R-02 (flagged)
The 10-K cover page and the earnings-release boilerplate describe "approximately 2,135 restaurants across 21 states" while Item 1 states 2,136 across 22 states. A one-unit/one-state difference between two documents filed the same day. Both are published; the site uses 2,136 (Item 1) and flags the discrepancy.
