# T3 — Regulatory, tax and labour-cost risk

## Source H — Measure ULA (City of Los Angeles)
URL: https://cityattorney.lacity.gov/homelessness
Verified text: "Measure ULA adds a **4% tax on property sales (residential and commercial) of more than $5 million, and a 5.5% tax on sales over $10 million**. Measure ULA revenue is earmarked for creating affordable housing and preventing more people from falling into homelessness."
Corroborating official statements: https://cd4.lacity.gov/press-releases/cd4release_measureula_022223/ ("4% tax on sales/transfers of real property exceeding $5 million ... 5.5% tax on sales/transfers of real properties of $10 million or more, with exceptions").
**Unverified:** the current CPI-indexed dollar thresholds for 2026. Third-party pages give at least two different pairs ($5.3M/$10.6M and $5.4M/$10.9M). No official page stating the current indexed thresholds was located, so no threshold other than the statutory $5M/$10M is published.

## Source I — Proposition 13 / Proposition 8 (Los Angeles County Assessor)
URL: https://assessor.lacounty.gov/tax-relief/decline-in-value
Verified: "Proposition 13 established the base year value for property tax assessment. It also caps the growth of a property's assessed value at **no more than two percent a year** unless the market value ... falls below the base year value." Prop 8 "allows a **temporary** reduction in assessed value when a property suffers a 'Decline-in-Value'." Filing window (per assessor contest page, https://assessor.lacounty.gov/homeowners/contest-assessment): **July 2 through November 30**.

## Source J — Fast-food minimum wage (California Department of Industrial Relations)
URL: https://www.dir.ca.gov/AB1228/AB1228.html
Verified: "AB 1228 ... raises the hourly minimum wage rate for certain fast food workers to **$20 effective April 1, 2024**. In addition, the legislation establishes a Fast Food Council within DIR to establish an hourly minimum wage for fast food restaurant employees".
**Unverified:** whether the Council has raised the rate for 2025/2026 and by how much. DIR's page does not state a subsequent figure; nothing is published on the site beyond the verified $20 / 2024-04-01 fact.
