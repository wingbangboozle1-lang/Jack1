Valuation & projection

A deterministic engine: cap rates as a function of lease term, anchored to a verified risk-free rate, with every assumption published.

Valuation engine

Value is income divided by a cap rate. Everything else is an argument about the cap rate.

The cap rate is not a constant. It is a function of how many years of rent the buyer can count on, and of what the risk-free rate is that day. So the engine models two things explicitly: a term structure (shorter lease, higher cap) and a rate sensitivity (verified 10-year Treasury of 4.90% as the anchor, with a ±75bp band).

Every assumption in the engine

If you disagree with the model, this is where to find it. Property-specific inputs were not supplied, so they are assumptions with a stated basis, not data.

KeyAssumptionValueBasis
A1Illustrative NOI cases$140,000 / $180,000 / $240,000Assumption. Actual NOI was not supplied. Chosen to bracket a plausible single-tenant QSR footprint in Central LA. Replace with the real figure before acting.

A8Discount rate / required return7.40%Assumption = verified 10-year Treasury 4.90% + 250bp illiquidity and single-tenant premium.
A9Cost of sale — brokerage, legal, closing5.0% of gross priceAssumption. Negotiated, not published.
A10Cost of sale — transfer taxes0.56% of price + Measure ULA where applicableDerived from verified rates: 0.45% city (S13) + 0.11% county (S14), then the ULA tiers.
A11Downtime if the tenant vacates12 months no rent + $150,000 re-tenanting + 6 months of free rentAssumption. Represents the realistic cost of re-leasing a drive-thru box.
A12Renewal rent reset+10% versus in-place rent at renewalAssumption. Six years of 2% contract growth against 3% market growth implies an 8-10% gap by 2032.
A13Combined income tax on a saleNot modelledThe owner's basis and depreciation history are private and were not supplied. All proceeds below are PRE-TAX. Tax is discussed qualitatively with the verified 1031 mechanics (F088).
A14Horizon2026-2035 (9 years)Chosen to cover the whole 2030-2035 expiry window stated by the owner.
A15Renewal concession (TI / rent abatement)$150,000Assumption. A tenant rarely signs an extension for nothing; the model charges the owner for it rather than pretending the extension is free.
A16Probability the tenant renews at expiry if nothing is negotiated60% (band 35%-75%)Assumption, informed by the franchisor's disclosed contraction (50-60 closures a year, mostly franchise) set against a lease that is likely below market after years of 2% escalators in a 3.7-4.1% inflation environment.
