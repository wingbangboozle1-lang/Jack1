Property & hazards

Eleven private facts are doing all the work.

The recommendation on this site depends on facts that are not public and were not supplied: the lease, the counterparty, the rent, the options, the basis and the parcel. Those fields are marked [WITHHELD] or left blank. This page sets out exactly what is needed and why each item changes the answer, so the owner can assemble it in one pass.

What the box actually isA 1,372 sq ft prototype with a Y-lane drive-thru on a commercially zoned parcel. The building is not the value; the land and the conditional-use entitlement for a drive-thru are.
What the lease actually isAn absolute-net lease shifts tax, insurance and maintenance to the tenant while the term runs. It does not shift them forever; after expiry they come back.
What the parcel actually isUnknown for hazard purposes. The Fire Hazard Severity Zone, the Alquist-Priolo status and the flood zone are all parcel-level queries that require an address.

How to read the tail

Three channels, three different answers

  1. Direct physical damage. A single-storey, 1,372 sq ft box on a surface parking field is at the resilient end of building stock. Shaking damage is real but unlikely to be structural collapse. The mitigation is the tenant's insurance and the lease's casualty clause.
  2. Business interruption. A closed store stops generating sales, and a sales-based percentage-rent clause (if any) stops paying. Under a true NNN lease, base rent normally continues, and the tenant — not the landlord — carries the interruption cover. Confirm this in the lease.
  3. Insurance-market contagion. This is the channel that actually threatens value. The Palisades Fire did not touch central Los Angeles, but a regional event reprices cover across the region, and the Department of Insurance itself describes insurers withdrawing as FAIR Plan enrolment grows. A parcel that cannot be insured cannot easily be sold or financed.
The check that costs the least and tells you the most Get a current commercial property insurance quote for the parcel this month. Under an NNN lease the tenant pays it, so the quote is diagnostic rather than a cost to the owner — and it is the fastest way to find out whether this asset is in a market at all.
