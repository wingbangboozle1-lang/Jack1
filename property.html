What the analysis needs from you (internal use only)

  • Executed lease plus all amendments and guaranties.
  • Tenant's certificate of insurance and most recent financial statement.
  • Current assessed value and property tax bill.
  • Any existing loan, its rate, maturity and prepayment terms.
  • Your basis and any intent to reinvest (drives the 1031 analysis).
