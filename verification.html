What a "verified" badge does and does not mean

It means the number or date shown was read at the linked official source on 2026-11-07 and matched. It does not mean the source is right about the future, that the geography is the subject property's geography, or that the figure will still be current tomorrow. Area statistics remain area statistics: none of them has been converted into a site forecast, an appraisal, or a rent estimate.
