Projection System — Specification, Inputs & Results

A documented, reproducible model built only from official inputs, with every unverifiable input declared, gridded and labelled. Normalised to an index: current asset value = 100.0.

Read the unit convention first. The subject property's value, rent and NOI were never supplied and cannot be verified from any official source. The model therefore runs entirely on a NORMALISED INDEX in which the asset's value on the retrieval date (2026-11-06) equals 100.0. All outputs are index points, not dollars. No dollar figure for the subject property appears anywhere on this site.
No official cap rate exists. No federal, state or local government agency publishes capitalisation rates for single-tenant net-lease quick-service-restaurant real estate. Cap rate therefore enters this model ONLY as an explicitly labelled, UNVERIFIED scenario input, expressed as a spread over the official U.S. Treasury par yield. Every output that depends on it is labelled UNVERIFIED INPUT.

Reproducibility. This file is the complete specification. Every number the Projection page displays is recomputed from these inputs by tools/model.py at build time. Nothing is hand-entered into the rendered tables. Running python3 tools/model.py regenerates data/projection_output.json, and python3 tools/build_site.py re-renders the page from it.

Fairness rules applied to both sides of the comparison

  1. Both paths bear the same statutory transaction cost: the sell path up front, the hold path on its terminal sale.
  2. The hold path additionally bears a vacancy/downtime scenario after expiry, because a NNN landlord resumes tax, insurance and maintenance on expiry and may not re-let immediately.
  3. The sell path's certain net proceeds are not discounted further. Net proceeds either earn the official Treasury risk-free rate or are reinvested in an identical asset at the same capitalisation rate; both treatments give a present value equal to the net proceeds.
  4. Tenant non-renewal is run in two regimes: the official system-wide base rate, and a 'signal received' regime at 100%. The second is what the recommended decision path exists to detect early.

Equations

IDNameFormulaNote
EQ-1NormalisationV0 = 100.0 index points = asset value on the retrieval date (2026-11-06)All outputs are relative to V0. No dollar figure for the subject property is produced anywhere.
EQ-2Capitalisation rate and opening net operating incomecap0 = risk_free_10y/100 + cap_spread_bp/10000 ; NOI0 = V0 x cap0Follows from V = NOI / cap. At the central spread of 200 bp on the official 4.84% ten-year par yield, cap0 = 6.84% and NOI0 = 6.840 index points. cap_spread_bp is UNVERIFIED input UX-1; risk_free_10y is official input IN-1. This is the only place a capitalisation rate enters the income side.
EQ-3Income path while tenantedNOI_t = NOI0 x (1 + g)^t for t = 1 .. ng is the rent-escalation scenario: 0% (UX-4, unverified, lease not supplied), or 2.9% / 3.4% / 3.7% from the official BLS Los Angeles CPI-U twelve-month readings at IN-3, IN-4 and IN-5.
EQ-4Tenant non-renewal probability at expiry (base regime)p_dark(n) = 1 - (1 - closure_rate_ongoing/100)^nCompound survival at the company-disclosed ongoing annual closure rate of 1% of system units (IN-9). n = 4 gives 3.94%; n = 6 gives 5.85%; n = 9 gives 8.65%. The JACK on Track programme-level bounds of 6.84% to 9.13% (IN-10, IN-11) are published alongside as the adverse case. Neither is site-specific: no public disclosure identifies individual restaurants selected for closure (flag F-31).
EQ-5Terminal value at expiry, net of the exit transaction costTV_gross = (1 - p_dark) x [NOI_n x (1 + g) / cap_exit] + p_dark x [V0 x (residual/100) x (1 + hpi/100)^n] ; TV_net = TV_gross x (1 - exit_cost)Two branches. The renewing branch re-capitalises the next year's income at cap_exit. The dark branch retains only the land and shell residual (UX-2, unverified) grown at the official FHFA rate (IN-6 central, IN-7 downside). exit_cost is the same Measure ULA rate charged to the sell path, applied here to the terminal sale.
EQ-6Discount rated = cap0The model discounts at the same rate it capitalises, so the present value of a level perpetuity equals its capitalised value exactly. This is deliberate: it isolates the effect of GROWTH and of TENANT OUTCOME from the effect of the rate level, so the comparison between paths is not an artefact of a chosen discount rate. The integrity check on the page verifies this identity at every point of the spread grid.
EQ-7Present value of the hold-to-expiry path (Option C)PV_hold = SUM_{t=1..n} [NOI_t / (1+d)^t] + TV_net / (1+d)^(n + downtime)downtime is the post-expiry vacancy scenario in years (0, 1 or 2), applied only to the terminal event. It is a scenario input, not an official statistic (flag F-33). Reported in index points per 100 of current value.
EQ-8Present value of the sell-now path (Option A)PV_sell = V0 x (1 - transaction_cost)transaction_cost takes the values 0% (parcel outside the City of Los Angeles, or price below the ULA threshold), 4.0% (IN-16) or 5.5% (IN-17). No further discounting is applied: net proceeds either earn the official Treasury risk-free rate or are reinvested in an identical asset at the same capitalisation rate, and both treatments return a present value equal to the net proceeds. County and City documentary transfer tax is additional but unverified (flag F-23) and is excluded rather than estimated, so PV_sell is OPTIMISTIC by roughly half a percent.
EQ-9Present value of the extend-then-hold path (Option B)PV_extend = EQ-7 evaluated at n + 10 years, with p_dark recomputed at n + 10Ten additional years of firm term is a scenario assumption. The extension is assumed to be granted at existing economics, which is not a forecast: the tenant has no obligation to negotiate early and is led by an interim chief executive (T-15, RK-21).
EQ-10Decision ruleChoose the path with the higher PV, then test whether that ranking survives the whole unverified-input gridBecause the capitalisation-rate input is unverified, the RANKING of paths is the output that matters, not the absolute index level. Where the ranking holds at every point of the Treasury-anchored spread grid and at every transaction-cost case, the conclusion is robust. Where it flips, the page says so explicitly rather than choosing a favourite spread.
EQ-11Catastrophe severity overlayfire_severity_share = fire_output_loss / la_county_gdp_2024IN-13 and IN-14 over IN-15 give 0.64% to 1.23% of one year of county economic output for a January-2025-class event. Reported as a severity band and deliberately NOT multiplied into any present value, because no official source maps a county output loss onto a specific commercial parcel's value.
EQ-12Earthquake frequency overlayp_eq_5yr_compound = 1 - (1 - eq_m67_30yr/100)^(5/30) ; p_eq_5yr_linear = eq_m67_30yr/100 x 5/30IN-12 gives 14.16% compound and 10.00% linear. Both are published because the restatement method changes the answer. The USGS figure is itself a 30-year probability from a physical model; restating it over a shorter window is arithmetic, not a new geophysical forecast.
EQ-13Signal regime — hold after the tenant has refusedPV_signal = EQ-7 with p_dark set to 1.0Once the tenant declines an extension, or discloses a franchise agreement expiring at or before the lease expiry, site-specific non-renewal moves from the system-wide base rate toward certainty and the hold path loses its renewing terminal branch. This is the regime the recommended plan is designed to detect years early, and it is where the ranking between hold and sell flips.
EQ-14Stress case — the conditions under which the recommendation is wrongPV_stress = EQ-7 with g = 0 (UX-4), residual = min(UX-2), hpi = IN-7, downtime = max scenario, exit_cost = 0, compared against PV_sell at transaction_cost = 0Built only from inputs already declared in this specification. Under that combination selling now wins even with no adverse tenant signal. Published because a recommendation that cannot state the conditions under which it is wrong is not a recommendation.
EQ-15Measure ULA dead bandnet(P) = P for P <= T ; net(P) = P x (1 - rate) for P > T ; therefore net(P) > net(T) requires P > T / (1 - rate)At the official 4% rate (IN-16) the break-even is 1.0417 x T, so a price up to 4.17% above the threshold nets LESS than a sale at the threshold. At 5.5% (IN-17) the break-even is 1.0582 x T, or 5.82% above. Pure arithmetic on the official statutory rates; no market assumption is used. The dollar thresholds themselves are unverified (flag F-24, irregularity IR-07) and are not stated anywhere.

Limitations

  1. No dollar value, rent, NOI or cap rate for the subject property was supplied or is verifiable from an official source. The model is normalised to an index and its outputs must be read as RELATIVE comparisons between decision paths, never as valuations.
  2. The cap-rate spread grid (UX-1) is unverified. No government agency publishes net-lease cap rates. Outputs that depend on it are labelled accordingly on every rendered table.
  3. The dark-box land residual grid (UX-2) is unverified. The only official land-versus-building ratio available (IN-18, 8.79%) is a national historical book-cost figure and is not used as a market estimate.
  4. The expiry date is a window, 2030 to 2035, not a date (UX-3). Results are shown for 4, 6 and 9 years.
  5. The lease itself was not supplied: escalator, options, expense stops, exclusive use and continuous operation are all unknown (F-02). The model tests escalation scenarios rather than asserting a lease structure.
  6. SCAG's adopted 2024 forecast and its 2025 draft cycle disagree on the direction of Los Angeles County population change (IR-02). The model does not use either as a driver; population enters only through the qualitative risk register.
  7. BEA has discontinued metropolitan-area GDP (E-35), so no MSA-level income projection is possible. County GDP is used instead.
  8. FEMA National Risk Index county values could not be read from fema.gov in this session (H-31 to H-34) and are not used as model inputs.
  9. The model contains no forecast of Jack in the Box Inc. financial performance beyond what the company has itself disclosed. Guidance is taken as disclosed and is not extrapolated.
  10. The post-expiry downtime scenario (0, 1 or 2 years) has no official basis; no government source quantifies quick-service-restaurant re-tenanting vacancy. It is included because omitting it would flatter the hold path, and the cash cost of carrying tax, insurance and maintenance during downtime is still NOT modelled, so the hold figures remain optimistic in a second way.
  11. The stress case at EQ-14 identifies conditions under which the recommendation reverses. Those conditions are published on the Projection page and in the Executive Summary rather than suppressed.
