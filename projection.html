Decision support / Projection system
Evidence snapshot · 08 Nov 2026

Reproducible model · verified drivers · break-even arithmetic

Project the area from official series; project the asset only from its own lease.

This page separates two questions that are often merged into one. Question one: what do published official series say about population, employment, income, prices, and rates in Los Angeles County through the lease-expiry window? Question two: how would any given property value respond to those drivers? Question one is answered with cited numbers. Question two is answered as arithmetic on stated parameters — never as an invented property value.

Model boundary

No rent, cap rate, building area, parcel, lease document, or owner tax position for the subject property has been verified. Nothing on this page is an appraisal, a forecast of this asset's value, or a projection of the tenant's store-level sales. Every "derived" figure below is arithmetic on the cited official number, and the formula is shown so it can be recomputed by hand.

Step 3 · transmission

How area drivers reach a NNN asset — and where the chain breaks

Link 1 · verified

Area population and income → potential demand

Census and SCAG publish population, income, and employment. What they establish is the size and direction of the local customer base, at county or city scale.

Link 2 · not verified

Demand → this restaurant's sales

Trade area, drive-time, competitors, daypart mix, and unit-level sales are private. No published series substitutes for them. This link is the largest single gap in the chain.

Link 3 · not verified

Sales → rent coverage and renewal capacity

Occupancy cost as a share of sales determines whether the operator can absorb an escalated renewal rent. Wage floors of $18.42/hour locally and $20.00/hour statewide raise the operating-cost baseline that coverage must clear.

Link 4 · arithmetic

Rent and expenses → net operating income

Under a true NNN structure most operating costs are the tenant's. Landlord exposure is then concentrated in structure, roof, parking, capital items, vacancy, and uncollectible rent — all lease-defined.

Link 5 · arithmetic

NOI and cap rate → value

Value = NOI ÷ cap rate. Because NOI is unknown here, the model below works in ratios: how much value changes for a given rent growth and cap-rate change, independent of the dollar amount.

Link 6 · contract

Lease term → buyer pool and price

A single-tenant asset with only a few years of remaining term is priced and financed as a re-tenanting proposition. This is the central reason remaining term, not area growth, drives the sell-versus-renew decision.

Step 4 · break-even arithmetic

What rent growth is needed just to stand still?

If NOI grows at g per year for n years and the exit cap rate is c₁ against an entry cap of c₀, then the value ratio is (1+g)n × (c₀ ÷ c₁). Holding value flat requires g = (c₁ ÷ c₀)1/n − 1.

Step 6 · transaction arithmetic

Transfer tax depends on price and closing date, not on the decision

If the parcel is inside the City of Los Angeles, the City transfer tax is 0.45% of consideration, plus Measure ULA of 4.0% above $5,400,000 and 5.5% at or above $10,900,000 for transactions closing after June 30, 2026. Thresholds are indexed annually to chained CPI, so a future sale must use the thresholds in force on that date.

Step 7 · model integrity

What would change the projection

Inputs that would move the answer most

  1. Actual remaining lease term and option rent. Term drives the buyer pool; option rent drives renewal economics. Neither is verified.
  2. Unit-level sales and occupancy cost. Determines whether the tenant can absorb an escalated rent and whether it will exercise any option.
  3. The parcel. Zoning, access, lot size, hazard zone, and insurance terms change the re-tenanting and redevelopment case.
  4. Owner basis and financing. After-tax proceeds and debt payoff can reverse a pre-tax conclusion.

Deliberate modelling choices

  • No cap rate is asserted for this property or for the submarket: no official county-level net-lease cap-rate series exists that can be tied to this asset.
  • No probability is assigned to any scenario: the official sources publish estimates and forecasts, not probabilities of area outcomes.
  • No inflation adjustment is applied to the sensitivity cells: they are nominal ratios, and the CPI series is shown separately.
  • Forecast conflict is preserved: the Census decline and the SCAG forecast both appear, and neither is averaged into the other.

Reproducibility

Every derived number on this page can be recomputed from the linked values: CAGRs use (end ÷ start)1/years − 1; annualised hazard probability uses 1 − (1 − p)1/30; break-even growth uses (c₁ ÷ c₀)1/n − 1; the sensitivity cells use (1+g)n × (c₀ ÷ c₁). The same formulas, with the same inputs and the results, are recorded in the repository verification log for this pass.
