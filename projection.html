Decision support / Projection model
Evidence snapshot · 07 Nov 2026

Projection system · official growth anchors · auditable arithmetic

A projection you can recompute by hand.

This model is built the only way an area model can be built honestly without a parcel: every growth rate comes from a published official forecast, every derived rate is shown with the arithmetic that produced it, and the property-level inputs are left for you to type because they are not public.

What this model refuses to do

It does not produce a sale price, a market rent, an appraisal, a cash-flow forecast or a recommendation for this asset. Those need the executed lease, the rent roll, the site, the condition report and the owner's tax position — none of which are public. A model that output a value anyway would be inventing it. What follows is the defensible part: official area growth, contractual rent arithmetic, and the explicit list of what is still missing.

Layer 4

The inputs that decide the outcome are not in this model

Because they are not public. Each one is a document request, not a research gap.

Contract

Lease and rent

Executed lease, all amendments, estoppels, rent ledger, options and notice windows. Without these the escalation input above is a placeholder, not a forecast.

Transfers

Value and proceeds

Independent valuation or real bids, loan payoff, brokerage and closing costs, and — inside City of LA — the transfer tax measured at closing. Current ULA rules.

Resilience

Hazard, coverage, condition

Parcel-level hazard overlays, insurance quotes and exclusions, roof and equipment condition, and seismic or retrofit obligations. See the risk register.
