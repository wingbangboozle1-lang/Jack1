Reading: 408,026 students across 784 schools with 21,246 classroom teachers implies a 19.20 student-to-teacher ratio, and current expenditure of $22,606 per student. Those figures describe the district, not a neighbourhood. For a restaurant site the school system matters in two concrete ways: it is a large public-sector employer (education and health services is the metro's fastest-growing sector at +3.8%), and it is a source of family household demand. Neither effect can be sized without the parcel.

Academic performance is not published here: the California School Dashboard district report was not opened in this session, so SCH-10 is left blank and flagged rather than filled from a secondary summary. Assignment boundaries are address-specific (SCH-09).
