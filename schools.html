School systems — LA Unified & the central-LA corridor

Schools rarely decide a NNN retail sale on their own, but they shape who lives nearby, household formation, and evening/weekend QSR traffic. Data below is from the California School Dashboard / CDE (official) and LAUSD (district release).

What this means for the asset

  • District momentum is positive but from a low base. LAUSD ELA distance-from-standard improved to 17.8 points below (2025) from 28.2 (2024) and 32.4 (2023); districtwide 2025 CAASPP results are the highest ever districtwide (district release). College/Career readiness is 54.9% prepared and trending up (+3.8 pts).
  • Central-LA schools skew toward high-needs students (high EL share, high poverty). The five-by-five College/Career grid shows district placement MEDIUM-green (increased), with 152 schools spread widely (20 red → 39 blue). The specific schools near the parcel must be checked individually — assignment is address-dependent and the address is anonymized here.
  • For a QSR landlord, school quality binds weakly. It matters via neighborhood desirability and daytime/evening traffic (schools dismiss 2–4 pm — a QSR snack/dinner daypart), not via property tax or entitlement. Do not pay a premium for "school district" the way a multifamily/residential buyer would.
