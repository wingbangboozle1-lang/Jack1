Tenant health and what the lease actually protects

A single-tenant NNN asset is a bond with a building attached. Its value is the tenant's ability and willingness to keep paying. This page is built from the company's own filings, not from press coverage.

The counterparty on this specific lease is unknown

93% of the Jack in the Box system is franchised, so the operator of this unit is probably a franchisee and the guarantor is probably not the public company. That distinction is the difference between an investment-grade credit and a small business. It cannot be resolved without the lease, so it is open item U-03 — and it is the first document to pull.

What the filings say

The system is contracting on purpose

The "JACK on Track" plan, unveiled in 2025, has three legs: a restaurant closure programme, the sale of Del Taco, and cash acceleration (dividend discontinued, new company development cut back). In FY2025 the system opened 31 units and closed 86. FY2026 guidance is a count of 2,050–2,100 — roughly 20 openings against 50–100 closures, "most of which will be franchise restaurants".

Translation for a landlord: the tenant's own plan assumes it will have fewer restaurants next year, and the closures are concentrated in the franchise base — the base most likely to be your counterparty.

Sales are falling at every level

FY2025 system same-store sales −4.2% (company −3.7%, franchise −4.3%). Q4 was materially worse: −7.4% system, −7.6% franchise. Management attributes it to transaction declines and unfavourable mix, partly offset by menu price — i.e. fewer customers, higher prices. That is the classic pre-renewal warning pattern.

A franchisee with falling sales and a $20/hour statutory wage floor cannot easily absorb a rent reset upward. Plan for a flat-to-modest renewal rent, not market-plus.

The balance sheet is being repaired by selling assets

Del Taco was agreed to be sold (stock purchase agreement signed 15 October 2025) for an aggregate price of $115m in cash, subject to adjustments; the dividend is gone; Q4 Adjusted EBITDA fell to $45.6m from $65.5m. FY2026 guidance is $225–240m of Adjusted EBITDA — and explicitly includes "selling real estate" as a driver of franchise-level margin.

A company that is monetising real estate is also a company that is deciding which leases to keep. Ask directly, in writing, whether this unit is on the retention list.

Lease mechanics that matter more than the brand

Standard franchise terms are 20 years with a $50,000 initial fee and royalties generally 5.0% of gross sales. The current prototype is ~1,372 sq ft. A small building on a commercially-zoned parcel means a high land-value share — good for residual value, but also the reason a buyer will underwrite the land, not the building, once the term gets short.

A 1,372 sq ft prototype on a corner in central Los Angeles is a redevelopment play dressed as a restaurant. Price it as both.
