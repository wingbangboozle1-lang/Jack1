Tenant Deep-Dive — Jack in the Box Inc. (NASDAQ: JACK)

The single most important variable in this decision. Built from SEC filings (10-K FY2025, 8-K FY2026) and clearly-labeled industry reporting. Balance sheet is stressed; the company is actively closing restaurants and selling real estate.

Headline
JACK is a leveraged turnaround: total debt $1.5B with net debt/Adjusted EBITDA of 6.3x (Q3 FY2026), negative same-store sales, two CEO transitions in 6 months, a 150–200 restaurant “JACK on Track” closure program, and an active corporate real-estate-sale program ($26.7M YTD FY26; $35–45M expected). Its securitized notes trade on a 4.476% Series 2019-1 A-2-II tranche now being paid down. Franchisee-level risk matters more than corporate: 93% of units are franchisee-operated.

5 · What this means for a NNN landlord (bottom line)

Against holding to 2030–35

  • 6.3x net leverage; FY26 Adj. EBITDA guide cut twice ($235→$230M).
  • Same-store sales negative for multiple years; transaction counts falling.
  • Closure program can logically select this asset (150–200 + 50–100 in FY26).
  • Parent is itself exiting owned real estate — it is not a natural renewing tenant-buyer.
  • 93% franchised: actual tenant is likely a franchisee whose economics (AUV $1.874M, margins thin) decide renewal, not corporate.
  • Guarantee (if any) unverified; franchisee default leaves landlord with a dark box.

Supporting a disciplined renewal (only if all true)

  • Unit is profitable: AUV ≥ system average; occupancy cost ≤ 15% of sales.
  • ≥10-year extension signed with ≥2% escalations and corporate/parent guarantee.
  • Store selected OUT of closure list (get it in writing from franchisor).
  • Franchisee produces 24 months of sales + current balance sheet; estoppel delivered.
  • Insurance incl. earthquake, and NNN pass-throughs, current and compliant.
Judgment
The probability mass sits on “sell before expiry.” Renewal is a negotiated upgrade, not a default outcome — treat it as an option the Owner must actively purchase (via early, documented, guaranteed extension), not as the base case.
