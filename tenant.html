What the filings show

  • Revenue has fallen for two straight fiscal years: $1,692.3M (FY2023) → $1,571.3M (FY2024) → $1,465.3M (FY2025).
  • The company moved from profit to loss: +$130.8M in FY2023, then −$36.7M in FY2024 and −$80.7M in FY2025.
  • It refinanced in June 2026 with $500M of 7.624% senior secured notes whose anticipated repayment date is May 2031 — after which additional interest accrues if not repaid. That 2031 date sits inside your lease-expiry window.
  • Much of the collateral is the franchise system itself, including real estate assets, which is the structural reason a landlord's rent is exposed to the chain's refinancing.

What this does NOT tell you

Chain-level results do not prove that this store is weak. A long-tenured urban unit can be among the best in the system. Two things decide it, and both are on your side of the table: whether the lease is corporate or franchisee-guaranteed (Flagged F-07) and what the unit actually sells (Flagged F-12).
