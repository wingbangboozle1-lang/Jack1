The dominant variable

Jack in the Box Inc. is contracting: 86 closures in FY2025, 17 more in Q3 FY2026, and a system count down to 2,115. Its FY2025 10-K names a restaurant closure programme as a core plan pillar. The Q3 FY2026 release also reports higher franchisee bad-debt expense and franchise-level margin pressure partly from lower rent and royalty revenue — a direct credit signal for a net-lease landlord whose tenant may be a franchisee.

SEC filings are issuer-level. They do not identify this property, its legal tenant, guarantor, rent, unit sales, franchise agreement expiry or closure-list membership. Those cells are blank.
