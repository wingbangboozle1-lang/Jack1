Reading: same-store sales fell 7.4% in the fourth quarter - worse in franchise stores (-7.6%) than company stores (-5.3%) - and 4.2% for the year, with 86 closures against 31 openings. Adjusted EBITDA of $45.6 million in the quarter was down from $65.5 million a year earlier, and the dividend was discontinued. Franchisees carry the sales decline and the $20.00 California wage floor at the same time, which is the channel through which a landlord's rent coverage weakens.

The offsetting facts are equally verified: fiscal 2026 guidance keeps the brand at 2,050-2,100 restaurants with same-store sales of -1% to +1% and franchise-level margin of $275-290 million. A franchisor in that position is still collecting royalties and still needs its better locations. That is the definition of a negotiable credit.

Not published: the operator of the subject unit, any guaranty, the issuer's credit rating and any location-level sales (TEN-18 to TEN-20). No rating-agency page was opened for this brief, so no rating is stated.
