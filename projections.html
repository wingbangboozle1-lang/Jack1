Jack in the Box NNN decision brief — central Los Angeles area

Official projections, transparent arithmetic, gated property outputs.

DOF and SCAG paths are kept separate; derived values are labeled; missing property inputs produce blank outputs, never zero.

Method

Official DOF and SCAG projections are published separately. Derived columns are transparent arithmetic from cited official inputs, not official observations. No interpolation, agency blending, parcel extrapolation, cap-rate assumption, rent-growth assumption, or invented probability is used. Property outputs remain blank because required inputs are unverified.

Symbolic stress tests

Lease and ownership stresses without invented magnitudes

Magnitudes and probabilities stay blank until evidence exists. These formulas define what must be measured.
