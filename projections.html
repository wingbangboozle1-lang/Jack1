A projection system with published inputs and empty outputs

Private worksheet

Break-even calculator (runs in your browser only)

Nothing typed here leaves your device: there is no network request, no storage, and no logging. The calculator does not pre-fill any value, because none is known. Use it once you have the lease in front of you.

Enter your own figures and choose “Compute”. Results are illustrative arithmetic on your inputs, not a valuation or an official statistic.
Privacy-first: no personal information is collected, embedded, or published. Property identifiers are intentionally omitted.
