Recommendation: hold and secure the term now, with a hard pre-committed sell trigger

Do not sell immediately. Do not wait passively for the reported 2030–2035 expiry. Negotiate a longer, signed renewal while preparing a sale in parallel; trigger the sale if the renewal is not signed, the site appears on a closure list, the franchise agreement ends at or before lease expiry, or tenant credit support deteriorates. Never ride to expiry unrenewed.

This is a decision rule, not a property-specific verdict: the address, APN, lease, rent, guarantor, debt, appraisal and owner tax profile were withheld for privacy. Every asset-specific value on this site is deliberately blank and flagged. The rule is derived from two verified facts — the issuer is contracting (86 closures in FY2025, 17 in Q3 FY2026), while the regional economy is large, mixed and still growing (+0.5% nonfarm jobs, $2.272 trillion assessment roll).
