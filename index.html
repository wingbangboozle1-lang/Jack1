Immediate actions

What can be done without guessing

  1. Calendar every lease notice, option, estoppel, and assignment deadline from the executed lease.
  2. Identify the legal tenant and guarantor; obtain financials and default history.
  3. Order title, parcel/hazard/zoning match, condition report, insurance review, and tax-basis workup.
  4. Request executable sale pricing and extension terms; compare after-tax net proceeds with risk-adjusted hold value.
  5. Fund a re-tenanting fallback before expiry; keep sale and renewal options open until evidence supports a choice.
