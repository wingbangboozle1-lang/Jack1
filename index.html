Executive summary

What the evidence supports

  1. Do not treat “Central Los Angeles” as one market. The Census, SCAG regional forecast, and Downtown Community Plan use different geographies. None can be assigned to this restaurant without a parcel location. See scope and indicators.
  2. Long-run regional growth is positive in the official baseline, but not a site forecast. SCAG’s adopted 2024 plan projects Los Angeles County population and employment through 2050. Downtown planning expectations apply only inside the Downtown Plan boundary and are not guaranteed outcomes. Read the projection table.
  3. The brand does not identify the lease guarantor. Jack in the Box’s latest reviewed quarterly filing reports both company-operated and franchise-operated restaurants. The specific landlord counterparty and any parent guarantee remain unknown. See tenant context.
  4. Tail hazards are material to the decision, and only part of the county-scale evidence survives an official read. USGS/UCERF3 states a 60% probability of M ≥ 6.7 (46% for M ≥ 7.0, 31% for M ≥ 7.5) within 30 years in the Los Angeles region, re-read on the USGS page on 08 Nov 2026. FEMA's OpenFEMA declaration summaries count 88 federal disaster designations for Los Angeles County since 1969 — 61 fire, 10 flood, 8 severe storm — counted directly through the federal API on 08 Nov 2026. County National Risk Index scores and expected annual loss are still withheld: they live inside FEMA's interactive tools and are not served by OpenFEMA. The reported 26% expansion of CAL FIRE Very High zones still could not be located on a state page. All of this is county-scale screening, not a parcel finding. Open the risk register.
  5. The regional economy is large, but the local population base is shrinking and parcel-level demand is unverified. Los Angeles County GDP was $1.003 trillion in 2024, up from $956.2 billion in 2023 in current dollars (BEA via FRED). The metro-area series is officially discontinued after 2023 at $1.295 trillion; a $1.355 trillion "2024" figure that appeared in an earlier draft could not be confirmed and is disclosed on Flagged for Review rather than published. City population fell 0.8% and county population 3.2% from the 2020 estimates base to July 2025, and county unemployment averaged 5.6% over the eleven published months of 2025. Ranking claims are avoided: no official source read for this brief ranks the county against other counties. These figures give macro context, not restaurant demand at this site. See area indicators.

Provisional course of action
Preserve any existing contractual options and notice rights while confirming them, and prepare both a sale case and a written renewal alternative. Do not waive rights, sign an extension, or accept a sale price until after-tax net proceeds and risk-adjusted hold value can be compared using verified property and lease inputs.

Recommended default: dual-track, sell-weighted.
Prepare and price a sale now, negotiate a qualifying renewal in parallel, and sign nothing until both cases are underwritten. Unless a renewal clears every test in Gate 3, sell with the lease in place before the final 24 months of term — i.e. between 2028 and 2033 depending on the true expiry date, with the listing window opening in late 2027.

Why this default and not “wait and see”: the option to renew is the tenant's, not the owner's. Jack in the Box discloses that its real-estate leases are generally 20-year initial terms “exclusive of options to renew, which are generally exercisable at our sole discretion for 1 to 20 years.” An owner who plans around a renewal is planning around a decision a third party controls. The same filing shows roughly 93% of the brand's restaurants are franchise-operated, so the paying obligor at this site may be a franchisee rather than the public company. Both facts are issuer-wide context, not findings about this parcel — but they make “renewal will happen” the weakest assumption in the file.

Analyst judgement, clearly separated from sourced data: the gates, the “sell-weighted” tilt, and the 24-month floor are this brief's own risk-balanced framework.
