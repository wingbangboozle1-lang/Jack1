Executive summary

What the evidence supports

  1. Do not treat “Central Los Angeles” as one market. The Census, SCAG regional forecast, and Downtown Community Plan use different geographies. None can be assigned to this restaurant without a parcel location. See scope and indicators.
  2. Long-run regional growth is positive in the official baseline, but not a site forecast. SCAG’s adopted 2024 plan projects Los Angeles County population and employment through 2050. Downtown planning expectations apply only inside the Downtown Plan boundary and are not guaranteed outcomes. Read the projection table.
  3. The brand does not identify the lease guarantor. Jack in the Box’s latest reviewed quarterly filing reports both company-operated and franchise-operated restaurants. The specific landlord counterparty and any parent guarantee remain unknown. See tenant context.
  4. Verification changed three things on this site. Every published figure was re-opened at its official source on 07 November 2026: one value was wrong and has been corrected, and ten that could not be reproduced were withdrawn rather than kept with a caveat. See the verification log and the withdrawn list.
  5. Tail hazards are material, but the county scores are no longer published here. Regional seismic probability is verified: USGS/UCERF3 puts a 60% chance on an M ≥ 6.7 event in the Los Angeles region within 30 years. FEMA\u2019s National Risk Index no longer displays a county score at its public entry point, and the CAL FIRE zone-expansion percentage could not be reproduced, so both were withdrawn rather than softened. Parcel-level mapping is still required. Open the risk register.
  6. The regional economy is large, but parcel-level demand is unverified — and two GDP figures were withdrawn. Los Angeles city population has fallen 0.8% since 2020 and county unemployment averaged 5.7% in 2025 (BLS annual county series). The metro and county GDP levels previously quoted here cited a labour series rather than GDP, so they are withdrawn pending a direct BEA read. None of this establishes restaurant demand at this site. See area indicators.

Provisional course of action
Preserve any existing contractual options and notice rights while confirming them, and prepare both a sale case and a written renewal alternative. Do not waive rights, sign an extension, or accept a sale price until after-tax net proceeds and risk-adjusted hold value can be compared using verified property and lease inputs.

Not a personalized wealth or tax recommendation

The owner’s basis, debt, tax position, liquidity needs, portfolio concentration, and risk tolerance are not known and are not requested or published. Those private factors can change the best individual decision.
