Recommended course of action: Negotiate a long renewal first, then sell the re-rated asset (recommended)

Pursue a long-term renewal first, then decide whether to sell the re-rated asset or hold. Sell only after the extension is executed (or, if the tenant refuses a renewal on acceptable terms, sell into the current liquid market rather than holding to expiry).

OPT-C keeps the asset in a low-risk, income-producing state while preserving an exit at a higher price. It converts an irreversible decision (sell now at the shortest WALT) into a reversible one (renew, then choose). That is the definition of preserving wealth with optionality.
