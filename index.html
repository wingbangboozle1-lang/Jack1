Executive summary

What the evidence supports

  1. “Central Los Angeles” is not one market. The Census, SCAG, and the Downtown Community Plan each use a different geography, and none can be tied to this restaurant without a parcel location. Compare the geographies.
  2. The official long-run baseline is modestly positive at county scale. SCAG's adopted Connect SoCal 2024 tables put Los Angeles County at 10,046,000 residents and 5,031,000 jobs in 2019, rising to 10,793,000 and 5,461,000 by 2050. See the projection system.
  3. The county is losing population while the labour market has tightened. Census estimates show the county down 3.2% since the 2020 base, while the county unemployment rate fell from a 5.8% 2024 annual average to 4.5% in September 2026. Labour detail.
  4. Inflation is still running above the pre-2021 pattern. The Los Angeles area CPI-U rose 3.7% over the 12 months to September 2026; food away from home rose 2.8%. This is the reference rate for any CPI-linked rent step. Price indicators.
  5. Tail hazards are real at county scale, but nothing is parcel-mapped. USGS gives a 60% chance of M ≥ 6.7 in 30 years in the Los Angeles region. CAL FIRE's published acreage table shows 769,561 acres of Very High fire-hazard zone in Los Angeles County across both responsibility areas. No parcel-level hazard determination has been made. Risk register.
  6. The decision cannot be settled on public area data alone. Rent, expiry, options, guarantor, value, debt and tax basis are all blank, and each one can flip the answer. Review the blanks.
✓

Provisional course of action
Confirm every notice right and option deadline first, then prepare a sale case and a written renewal alternative in parallel. Do not waive rights, sign an extension, or accept a price until after-tax net proceeds and a risk-adjusted hold value can be compared using verified property and lease inputs.

Not a personalised wealth or tax recommendation

The owner's basis, debt, tax position, liquidity needs, portfolio concentration, and risk tolerance are not known, are not requested, and are not published. Those private factors can change the best individual decision.
