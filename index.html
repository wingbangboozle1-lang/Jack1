The decision

What the evidence supports

  1. Renew rather than sell, on the evidence available. A triple-net lease with five to nine years remaining is the most valuable thing you own here: it converts tenant credit into contractual rent while you keep title. Selling converts that into cash at a price that depends on a capitalisation rate nobody has quoted you yet.
  2. The tenant is the credit, and the credit is weakening - but not broken. Systemwide same-store sales fell 7.4% in the fourth quarter and 4.2% for fiscal 2025, 86 restaurants were closed, and the dividend was discontinued. Against that, franchise-level margin is guided at $275-290 million for fiscal 2026 and restaurant count is guided to 2,050-2,100. This is a "negotiate now, monitor quarterly" credit, not a "sell in a panic" credit.
  3. The area economy is mild, not collapsing. Metropolitan employment grew 0.5% year over year while population fell -0.8% in the city and -3.2% in the county since 2020. Education and health services (+3.8%) is carrying growth; information (-5.7%) and financial activities (-1.7%) are shrinking.
  4. Transaction friction is unusually high in this jurisdiction, with a cliff. Los Angeles adds a 4.0% Measure ULA surcharge on a sale above $5,400,000 and 5.5% at $10,900,000 or more, on top of a 0.45% base tax. One dollar above the threshold costs about $216,000 more in tax. That is a reason to price a sale carefully and to negotiate the timing, not a reason to avoid selling forever.
  5. The black-swan exposures are real but unquantified for this site. The January 2025 Palisades fire destroyed 6,845 structures in this county; a modelled Mw 7.8 San Andreas event carries 1,800 deaths and $191 billion of regional economic loss. Neither is a probability for this parcel. Both are reasons to confirm insurance, lease allocation and resilience before you commit capital.

Scope and privacy

Public evidence only

This site publishes aggregated public statistics and the official page each one came from. It contains no owner name, street address, parcel number, ownership entity, lease document, rent figure, debt balance or personal financial detail, and it collects nothing from visitors: no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, no forms. Asset-specific fields that cannot be checked from public data are blank and listed on Flagged for review, together with the official lookup that would close each one.

Privacy: aggregated public statistics only. No personal identifiers are stored in this repository or served by this site.

Every table value links to the official page it was read from on 2026-11-07. Blank cells are unverified or withheld by policy — they are listed on Flagged for review. This is analysis for the owner, not appraisal, legal, tax or investment advice.
