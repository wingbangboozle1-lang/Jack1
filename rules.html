Why each one matters to this specific asset

  • Proposition 13 means selling resets the tax base to the sale price. A buyer knows that and prices it in; you should quantify it before you list.
  • Measure ULA means a sale above $5.4M costs 4.45% in city transfer tax, seller-paid. On a $6M sale that is $267,000 — a real number that changes the sell/hold arithmetic.
  • AB 1228 set a $20/hr fast-food floor and caps future increases at the lesser of 3.5% or CPI-W, with no Council-driven increase effective after 2029. The tenant's cost pressure is bounded, which supports renewal — but the pressure lands on the operator, so it lands on you at re-letting.
  • 1031 gives you a way to move the money without paying the tax, provided you accept a hard 45-day identification and 180-day closing clock.
  • Fire and fault zones are disclosure and insurability rules, not just physical risks: they can change what your buyer can finance.
