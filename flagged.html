Source irregularities found during verification

R-01 · Two official population series disagree by 158,501

U.S. Census Bureau, Vintage 2025: Los Angeles County = 9,694,934 (1 July 2025). California Department of Finance, Baseline 2024 / Vintage 2026: 9,853,435 for the same date. Different products, different methods, both official. Published as separate rows; never blended or averaged.

R-02 · The company disagrees with itself on its own store count

The FY2025 Form 10-K Item 1 states 2,136 restaurants across 22 states. The boilerplate in the Q4 earnings release issued the same day states "approximately 2,135 restaurants across 21 states". A one-unit, one-state discrepancy inside a single filing day. The site uses 2,136 (the audited business description) and flags the conflict.

A note on what "verified" means here

A row is marked verified only when the exact number was read on the cited official page during this session and transcribed without adjustment. Anything second-hand, inferred, rounded to fit, or remembered was excluded. Rows marked derived show arithmetic explicitly. Rows marked unverified carry a blank value — never an estimate.
