Flagged for Review

Every blank below is deliberate. Blank does not mean zero, immaterial, or unfavorable—it means that an official, parcel-specific source or private diligence document was not available. No missing value has been inferred.

Privacy guardrail

This public checklist does not ask for, store, or publish an owner’s name, exact address, APN, ownership entity, personal financial data, or private documents. Any sensitive diligence should remain outside the public website and be handled through an appropriately secure process.

Verification and publication limitations

The automated check verifies link structure and approved source domains, not that every cited page supports every statement or that external links are reachable. A complete independent line-by-line source audit remains open. For example, the city population and income figures on the summary were checked against Census QuickFacts; that check does not verify the remaining rows.

The repository is public, so unpublished or archived repository files are not private merely because they are omitted from the dashboard. Do not place personal information or private diligence documents in this repository. GitHub Pages reported an error for its latest legacy build even though a separate Actions deployment succeeded and the dashboard was reachable; the publication configuration needs review.

Closure criteria

What would change the conclusion?

Sale case becomes decision-ready when

  • Parcel/title and current lease are reconciled, including options, guaranties, defaults, and notices.
  • Independent valuation or real offers are compared against net proceeds after debt, taxes, and transaction costs.
  • Owner-specific tax and reinvestment options are reviewed with a qualified professional.
  • Condition, zoning, insurance, and environmental diligence are complete enough for disclosure and pricing.

Renew / hold case becomes decision-ready when

  • Renewal rent and term are compared with market evidence and documented tenant financial support.
  • Actual landlord expense and capital obligations are forecast through the full hold period.
  • Vacancy / re-tenanting alternatives and residual land-use constraints are independently assessed.
  • Risk-adjusted after-tax value is competitive with sale proceeds and meets the owner’s private objectives.
