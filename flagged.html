IR-01BEA has discontinued metropolitan-area GDP and personal income statisticsThe Bureau of Economic Analysis states in its 2024 county release that GDP and personal income statistics for metropolitan statistical areas, micropolitan areas, metropolitan divisions and combined statistical areas have been discontinued. Any projection that previously relied on Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim MSA GDP can no longer be sourced. This analysis was rebuilt on COUNTY-level GDP ($818.5 billion for Los Angeles County, 2024), which changes the geography of every income anchor used.U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis ↗Method change forced mid-analysis. Documented rather than hidden.
IR-02SCAG's newer forecast reverses the direction of its own adopted forecast for Los Angeles County populationThe adopted Connect SoCal 2024 plan, published December 2023, projected Los Angeles County population rising about 720,000 (+7%) to 10.77 million by 2050. The Connect SoCal 2050 Technical Working Group draft of November 20, 2025 projects county population DECLINING about 5% to 9.3 million by 2050 — while projecting county HOUSEHOLDS rising about 6%. Two official documents from the same regional agency disagree on the sign of the county's population trend. The projection model uses neither as a driver; population enters only through the qualitative risk register.Southern California Association of Governments ↗Long-range population forecasts for this region are not stable. Any 2050-horizon conclusion drawn from a single SCAG document is unsafe.

Verification standard. Every table row on this site carries a link to an official publisher — a government agency, a regulator, a central bank, a regional planning agency, or a document filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the value cell is left blank, the row is marked Unverified, and the reason is registered on the Flagged for Review page. No value on this site is inferred, estimated from memory, or taken from a news article, brokerage, aggregator or wiki.

Privacy. The subject property is deliberately anonymised. Its address, parcel number, owner identity, purchase price, rent, net operating income and insurance details are withheld and are not published anywhere on this site, including in the underlying data files. Every parcel-specific and owner-specific field is therefore blank by design and flagged as such.
