Closure criteria

What would change the conclusion?

Sale case becomes decision-ready when

  • Parcel/title and current lease are reconciled, including options, guaranties, defaults, and notices.
  • Independent valuation or real offers are compared against net proceeds after debt, taxes, and transaction costs.
  • Owner-specific tax and reinvestment options are reviewed with a qualified professional.
  • Condition, zoning, insurance, and environmental diligence are complete enough for disclosure and pricing.

Renew / hold case becomes decision-ready when

  • Renewal rent and term are compared with market evidence and documented tenant financial support.
  • Actual landlord expense and capital obligations are forecast through the full hold period.
  • Vacancy / re-tenanting alternatives and residual land-use constraints are independently assessed.
  • Risk-adjusted after-tax value is competitive with sale proceeds and meets the owner’s private objectives.
