Privacy

Withheld fields, never published

Internal use only

These fields are private. They are listed so the owner knows what they are, not so their values can be inferred. Nothing in this repository, its data files or its pages contains an owner name, a street address, a parcel number, an ownership entity, a rent figure, a loan figure, a cost basis or any personal financial detail.

I-07An earlier build labelled city poverty as a "2025 estimate". Defect corrected. The Census methodology note states sub-county poverty comes from ACS 5-year data, so the period is 2020–2024.

The rates and the current dollar thresholds are now sourced to the City of Los Angeles Office of Finance, which publishes them, and the thresholds are labelled as annually indexed.
