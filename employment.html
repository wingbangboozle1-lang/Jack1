Mixed, weak growth with a structural shift

Total nonfarm employment is growing slowly while the sector mix changes materially: education and health services are expanding and information is contracting. Weekday demand in office-heavy central corridors is therefore weaker than the headline suggests. The official EDD projection window ends in 2033; nothing is extrapolated beyond it.
