Analysis · projection model

Deterministic arithmetic - reproducible with python3 scripts/build_site.py

A value range you can test, not a valuation you can trust.

The appraised value, the rent and the capitalisation rate are not public. Rather than invent them, the model runs them as inputs and reports what the answer is for each combination, including the exact exit capitalisation rate at which holding stops beating selling.

What this means for the decision: the value question is not "what is it worth" but "what rate would a buyer pay, and do I know something about the rent that a buyer would pay more for?" Renewing at market rent with documented escalations is the only lever in your control that raises the numerator of that fraction.

This brief does not give tax advice; it flags the cliff and its verified rate.

Reading: holding wins unless the exit capitalisation rate is more than about 58 basis points wider than today's. Break-even

The exit rate at which holding stops paying

Break-even exit rate: the rate at which the discounted value of five years of rent plus a sale equals the net cash you would hold today. A wider cushion means holding is more robust to a repricing.

Bisection solution of the hold NPV in the Method section. Transfer-tax rates from LA Office of Finance↗.

Reading: the cheaper the entry price (a higher entry cap rate), the more room there is for rates to rise before selling would have been better. If a broker quotes you a 5.25% cap rate today, the break-even exit rate is only about 5.40% - almost no cushion. If the market is really at 7.25%, the break-even exit is 8.44% - a comfortable margin. Ask for the cap-rate evidence before you accept either.

Method

Formulas, so the arithmetic can be checked

Value: V = NOI / c

Net proceeds: P - (0.0045 + u(P)) x P - 0.02 x P, where u(P) = 0 above $5.4m, 0.04 above $5.4m, 0.055 at $10.9m or more

Hold NPV: sum over t = 1..5 of NOI x (1+g)^t / (1+d)^t + net proceeds of a sale at NOI x (1+g)^5 / c5, discounted 5 years

Break-even exit rate: solve for c5 where hold NPV equals net proceeds today (bisection, 200 iterations)

Limits. Pre-tax, unlevered, no capital expenditure, no vacancy, no lease-expiry event inside the five years, no transaction-cost inflation and no probability weighting. It is a sensitivity tool: it tells you which inputs matter and how much, not what will happen. A licensed appraiser, a tax adviser and a broker's opinion of value are required before any transaction.
