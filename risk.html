The four risk buckets

1 · Income risk (the one that decides the outcome)

Tenant credit, renewal timing and occupancy-cost capacity. A NNN lease transfers expenses to the tenant; it does not transfer vacancy. If the operator fails or declines to renew, the owner holds an empty 1,372 sq ft building on a commercial parcel. Everything else on this page is second-order compared with this.

2 · Exit-cost risk (certain, not contingent)

Measure ULA applies 4.0% of the full price above $5m and 5.5% above $10m, paid by the seller, on top of documentary transfer taxes and advisory fees. This is the only risk on the page that is certain to occur if you sell. It is therefore a cost to be optimised, not a risk to be managed.

3 · Cost-pass-through risk

Energy CPI +20.3% and insurance repricing in a state where the FAIR Plan is the insurer of last resort. In a NNN structure these land on the tenant's occupancy-cost ratio, which is precisely the number that determines whether the tenant can afford the rent at renewal.

4 · Catastrophe risk (low probability, severe)

Earthquake and wildfire are regional events with parcel-level consequences. The honest position: the parcel-level designations for this property are unverified (U-01, U-02), so the mitigation list below is a to-do list, not a reassurance.

Black-swan anchors

Seismic

The USGS/CGS ShakeOut scenario models a magnitude 7.8 rupture on the southern San Andreas Fault producing about 1,800 deaths and $213bn of economic losses, That is a scenario, not a forecast — but it is the planning basis used by the state and it is the reason earthquake cover and retrofit status are non-negotiable diligence items.

Wildfire

The January 2025 Palisades Fire burned 23,448 acres, destroyed 6,845 structures, damaged 975 and killed 12 people over 24 days; the cause is recorded as arson. Central Los Angeles was not in the burn area, but the second-order effects — insurance repricing, utility costs, regional demand shock — were metro-wide.

The two open hazard questions

U-01 — is the parcel in a CAL FIRE Fire Hazard Severity Zone, and at what tier? U-02 — is it in an Alquist-Priolo fault zone or a CGS seismic hazard zone, and does the building predate the retrofit ordinances? Both require the address in the official viewers. Both change insurance cost, and therefore the tenant's NNN load. Both are on the Flagged for Review page with direct links.
