## Official-source re-read — 2026-11-15

| Official source | Text checked | Decision-tool implication |

| [Los Angeles Office of Finance — Real Property Transfer Tax and Measure ULA FAQ](https://finance.lacity.gov/faq/measure-ula) | The page note states that transactions closing after June 30, 2026 use $5.4M and $10.9M thresholds; the operative rate table lists City base tax at $2.25 per $500 (or fractional part), ULA at 4% above $5.4M and below $10.9M, and ULA at 5.5% at or above $10.9M. The City-only table must not be mislabeled as the complete County-plus-City stack. The existing Flagged for Review item remains open. |
| [Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder — Documentary Transfer Tax](https://www.lavote.gov/home/recorder/property-document-recording/documentary-transfer-taxes/general-info) | County tax is $0.55 for each $500 or fraction thereof when taxable consideration/value exceeds $100; the County page describes its base as excluding liens/encumbrances remaining at transfer. Its City schedule lists Los Angeles effective July 1, 2026 with the $2.25/$500 City base and the corresponding 4% / 5.5% ULA tiers. It separately lists other special-city schedules. | The exact tax cannot be recovered from a single flat percent of gross sale price without confirmed tax bases, situs, exemptions, and transaction allocation. A title/escrow calculation is required for an executable sale estimate. |
| [IRS Publication 544 — Sales and Other Dispositions of Assets](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544) and [IRS Instructions for Form 8824](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf) | Official references for dispositions and like-kind exchange reporting; no individualized owner tax amount can be obtained from the public property description. | The browser tool should use an adviser-provided tax estimate or clearly label any calculation as a limited scenario, not present a generic blended-rate result as verified tax due. A 1031 deferral is not a tax-free wealth comparison. |

If the same taxable value is used for both jurisdictions, is divisible by $500, and the property is wholly within the City of Los Angeles, adding the County $0.55/$500 base to the City $2.25/$500 base gives a nominal 0.56% base stack; adding the ULA 4% or 5.5% yields commonly stated combined effective rates of 4.56% or 6.06%. That arithmetic is not an exact fee calculator for arbitrary transactions because both base taxes round by $500 and the tax bases can differ. This distinction is reflected in the official sources above and in the existing `dtt_combined_stack` derived row.
