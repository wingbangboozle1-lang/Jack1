## Material limits and outstanding decision gates

1. **No parcel identity:** exact site, city boundary, tract, parcel-level CAL FIRE/FEMA/USGS hazard status, zoning, school assignment, and local competitor/traffic evidence cannot be attributed without a secure parcel match. City/county context is not parcel evidence.
2. **No executed lease:** the 2030–2035 expiry is a reported range only. The executed lease, amendments, options, notice dates, rent steps, NNN responsibility, legal obligor, guaranty and assignment/default provisions are absent. A private lease abstract and calendar reconciliation remain essential.
3. **No executable property economics:** sale/renewal quotes, appraisal/BOV, debt payoff, transaction costs, rent roll, renewal concessions, title, capex, insurance terms, tax basis, depreciation, ownership entity, and owner hurdle rate are unknown. No sale or renewal winner can be computed.
4. **Worksheet is not a complete after-tax model:** the single blended sale tax rate does not separately compute federal/state capital gains, depreciation recapture, entity-specific treatment, NIIT, passive losses, exchange boot, or adviser-confirmed 1031 eligibility. The hold PV is a simplified rent-plus-exit discounted calculation; it does not model full lease-expiry vacancy, renewal-specific terms, tax on rents/exit, debt amortization, operating/capital costs, or hazard/insurance stress. It must not be represented as a back-tested, property-calibrated projection system.
5. **Future transfer-tax rates are unknown:** official ULA thresholds adjust annually. The browser worksheet uses the currently posted schedule for a scenario, not an invented 2030–2035 threshold. Recheck the official schedule for the proposed closing year.
6. **Privacy boundary:** browser-only inputs are not persisted by this app and the worksheet code makes no network call, but GitHub Pages is public and a browser/device, extension, screenshot, or shared computer is not an access-controlled adviser workspace. Keep identity, lease documents, parcel records, and sensitive owner finances offline with authorized advisers. The website contains no such identifiers.
7. **Historical/publication issues:** existing Flagged for Review entries remain the source of truth for the stale ULA narrative wording, Census tract-data/access gap, debt-schedule reconciliation, unresolved commit-metadata privacy review, and any other previously logged source discrepancies. This session does not silently close or supersede them.
