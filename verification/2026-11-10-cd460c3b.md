## 1. Material correction found

### F-01 — California Jack in the Box restaurant count was the **Del Taco** row

* **Published before this pass:** "California Jack in the Box restaurants, FY2025 — 351 total;
  94 company; 257 franchise" and derived "California share of Jack system 16.4%".
* **Read on the official page (2026-11-10):** Jack in the Box Inc. FY2025 Form 10-K, **Item 2.
  Properties**, table "restaurant locations (by segment, by state) for all restaurants in operation as
  of September 28, 2025":
  <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/807882/000080788225000072/jack-20250928.htm>

  | California | Del Taco Company-Operated | Del Taco Franchise | Del Taco Total | Jack in the Box Company-Operated | Jack in the Box Franchise | Jack in the Box Total |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | as printed | 94 | 257 | **351** | **96** | **845** | **941** |

  The published figures 351 / 94 / 257 are the **Del Taco** California row. The Jack in the Box
  California row is **941 total; 96 company-operated; 845 franchise**.
* **Cross-checks on the same table** (to confirm the row/column reading is correct and not a
  column shift): the table's totals line reads `132 | 444 | 576 | 150 | 1,986 | 2,136`, which
  reconciles with the 10-K narrative — "As of September 28, 2025, Jack in the Box operated and
  franchised 2,136 quick-service restaurants … Of those total Jack in the Box restaurants at
  fiscal year-end, 1,986, or 93%, were franchised" (150 company-operated) and "Del Taco operated
  and franchised 576 restaurants … 444, or 77%, were franchised" (132 company-operated).
* **Impact.** The published share was wrong by a factor of about 2.7 (16.4% instead of 44.1%).
  California is in fact the largest state market in the Jack in the Box system, which is material to
  a central-Los-Angeles landlord: the brand's California exposure, franchisee base and real-estate
  turnover are concentrated in the same state and market conditions as the subject site.
* **Action taken.** Corrected to 941 / 96 / 845 with derived share 44.1% (941 ÷ 2,136 = 44.06%).
  The prior values are disclosed on the Flagged for Review page so the change is auditable.
