## 3. Irregularities and notes

- **I-1 (already flagged, re-confirmed):** The ULA FAQ body text still says the ULA tax applies above
  "$5 million" and at "$10 million or greater". The note and rate table on the same page say $5.4 million
  and $10.9 million, effective for transactions after June 30, 2026. Use the note and table; the body text
  is outdated. Tracked as `flag_ula_faq_wording`.
- **I-2 (new flag):** The Treasury November table now shows the 11/12 and 11/13 par yields. On 11/13:
  2Y 4.88%, 5Y 4.98%, 10Y 5.15%, 20Y 5.52%, 30Y 5.47%. The dataset's 11/10 row is accurate as published and is
  dated, so it was left unchanged. Tracked as `flag_treasury_refresh_20261113`.
- **I-3 (note):** The ULA FAQ chart shows "applicable tax rates" of 4.45% and 5.95%. These are the city
  base plus ULA rate, without the county 0.11% documentary transfer tax. The dataset's combined stacks
  (0.56%, 4.56%, 6.06%) add the county tax. The county 0.11% figure was not re-read today.
- **I-4 (note):** The EDD release rounds levels to the nearest hundred. The dataset's LAUS levels
  (5,031,013 / 4,806,494 / 224,519) come from the BLS series page, which shows the unrounded figures.
