Evidence snapshot · 08 Nov 2026

Repository work · integrity findings · corrections applied

What the repository review found, and what was done about it.

This repository was built by many concurrent sessions. That produced strong content and also produced competing builds, orphaned pages, stale figures, unresolved merge markers, and two deployment paths racing each other. Each finding below is factual and checkable in the repository; each carries the action taken in this pass.

Nothing was deleted to make the review look clean

Earlier sessions' work is preserved. Where a page is superseded it is excluded from the published artifact and named here, rather than removed from history. Where two sessions published different figures, the correct one was re-read from the official source and the difference is recorded.

Privacy audit

Personal-information scan of everything published

Scanned and found clear

  • No email address or telephone number appears in the rendered text of any published page; the build check fails if one does.
  • No street address, parcel number, owner name, ownership entity, or guarantor identity appears on any published page.
  • No lease document, rent schedule, payoff statement, appraisal, or tax return is embedded, linked to a private store, or reproduced.
  • The only property description published is the one the brief supplied: a Jack in the Box NNN asset near central Los Angeles, expiring in a reported 2030–2035 window.

Deliberately withheld

  • Owner identity, ownership structure, and any personal financial position.
  • Parcel-level identifiers and any inference that could re-identify the asset from public records.
  • Tenant entity, franchisee identity, and guarantor status — unknown publicly in any case.
  • Anything supplied privately during the engagement; such material stays internal and is not represented on this site.
Residual risk: a determined reader could attempt to identify candidate Jack in the Box parcels in central Los Angeles from public listings. This site publishes nothing that narrows that set — no address, no APN, no assessed value, no unit-level sales — and the risk register treats location disclosure as a controlled risk rather than an absent one.
Privacy-first: no personal information is collected, embedded, or published. Property identifiers are intentionally omitted.
