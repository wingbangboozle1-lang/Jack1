Insurance

The tail that decides whether a hold is survivable

Insurance is the mechanism that converts an uninsurable tail into a manageable one - or fails to. This brief does not publish a premium, a deductible, a coverage limit or an availability conclusion for this parcel, because no policy was reviewed (PRP-17, CAT-17) and no California Department of Insurance page was opened in this session. Treat that as the single largest verification gap on this page: an owner who cannot bind affordable fire and quake cover is holding a different asset than the one modelled here.

The lease question is equally concrete: under a triple-net lease the tenant typically carries insurance and restoration, but "typically" is not a contract term. Read the insurance, damage-and-restoration and condemnation clauses before relying on them (PRP-10, CAT-18).
