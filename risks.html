Risk Register — Every Decision Path

Twenty-eight risks across six decision paths, each scored against an official anchor where one exists and labelled as judgement where one does not. Black-swan events are marked separately.

Scoring. Likelihood and Impact are each scored 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High). Score = Likelihood x Impact, 1 to 25. Bands: 1-4 Low, 5-9 Moderate, 10-14 Elevated, 15-19 High, 20-25 Severe.
Honesty rule. Where a likelihood is anchored in an official published statistic, the anchor is named in the 'basis' field and the row is marked OFFICIAL. Where no official statistic exists, the row is marked JUDGEMENT and is also listed on the Flagged for Review page. No judgement-based likelihood is presented anywhere on this site as a verified number.

A black-swan row is one where a low-probability event produces damage disproportionate to the asset's income, and where the loss is not diversifiable within a single-asset, single-tenant position.

Verification standard. Every table row on this site carries a link to an official publisher — a government agency, a regulator, a central bank, a regional planning agency, or a document filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the value cell is left blank, the row is marked Unverified, and the reason is registered on the Flagged for Review page. No value on this site is inferred, estimated from memory, or taken from a news article, brokerage, aggregator or wiki.

Privacy. The subject property is deliberately anonymised. Its address, parcel number, owner identity, purchase price, rent, net operating income and insurance details are withheld and are not published anywhere on this site, including in the underlying data files. Every parcel-specific and owner-specific field is therefore blank by design and flagged as such.
