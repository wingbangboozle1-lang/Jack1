Low-frequency / high-impact scenario treatment

“Black swan” is used here as a scenario-planning label—not a claim that a specific event is likely. Earthquake, wildfire, major flooding, prolonged utility interruption, insurance withdrawal, abrupt tenant failure, or regulatory change can be stress-tested only after confirming the site, coverage, lease allocation, and resilience measures. No numerical probability is invented.
