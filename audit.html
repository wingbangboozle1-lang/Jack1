Repository and record findings

Irregularities found in the record itself

These are defects in the repository, the citation trail, or the official record, not judgments about the property. Each is disclosed with what was done about it.

Non-blocking

F-01 · County labour-force levels could not be reproduced

Earlier revisions printed 5,148,900 / 4,889,600 / 259,300 for December 2025 Los Angeles County and cited a California EDD local-area page. That page now reports county labour-force data as unavailable, and the BLS county series prints 5,129,498 / 4,872,197 / 257,301 at a 5.0% rate. Action taken: values replaced with the BLS series and the superseded numbers disclosed on Flagged for Review. No value was inferred to fill the gap.

F-02 · A citation that no longer supports its own figure

A link that stays live but stops carrying the data is more dangerous than a dead link. Action taken: the county unemployment rows now cite the BLS LAUS county series directly, and the EDD page is recorded in the register above as evidence of the gap.

F-03 · Measure ULA thresholds were previously treated as unconfirmable

Earlier sessions could not confirm the indexed thresholds on an official page and withheld them. They are published on the City of Los Angeles Office of Finance FAQ: $5,400,000 and $10,900,000 for closings after 30 June 2026, at 4% and 5.5% on top of a 0.45% base rate. Action taken: reclassified from unverified to verified-with-expiry, because the thresholds re-index annually to the chained CPI and must be re-read at closing.

F-04 · Unresolved merge-conflict markers were committed to the coordination board

Three conflict-marker pairs from a parallel-session merge were committed into the repository's coordination file. Action taken: markers removed and both sessions' records retained; the audit coordinator is the only party that edits shared files.

F-05 · Conflict markers also sit inside an archived earlier build

An archived build directory retains conflict markers in its HTML and stylesheet. Action taken: none, deliberately — rewriting an archived artifact would destroy the evidence of what that session shipped. The Actions artifact excludes the archive, but the active legacy Pages source serves the repository root, so historical material remains publicly reachable. See Flagged for Review.

F-06 · An open pull request would break the deploy gate if merged as-is

A parallel branch replaces the root page set with differently named pages but does not update the validator, which requires the current seven page names. Merging it unchanged would fail the pre-deploy check. Action taken: the branch is left open for its own author to fix; it was not merged by this session and nothing from it was discarded.

F-07 · Several competing builds remain in the repository

Root pages, a pages directory, a site directory, and three archive directories hold different generations of this same analysis. Only the audited set is published. Action taken: retained for traceability; readers should treat any unpublished copy as superseded.

F-09 · A GDP figure cited to an unemployment series

Two GDP rows linked a labour-force identifier instead of a GDP series, so a reader following the link would have landed on county unemployment data. Action taken: both rows now link the county GDP series and the metro GDP series, whose published values are 1,002,965,029 thousand dollars for the county in 2024 and 1,295,361.4 million dollars for the metro area in 2023.

F-10 · A metro GDP year that was never published

A parallel session published a 2024 metro GDP figure. The official metro series is marked discontinued and stops at 2023. Action taken: the verified 2023 value is published, the 2024 claim is disclosed on Flagged for Review, and the unsupported ranking is dropped because it was not verified. A conflicting landing-page paragraph persisted until the 08 Nov 2026 correction.

F-11 · Hazard scores that exist but could not be read

Six county-level hazard figures arrived from a parallel session minutes before publication: the risk-index composite, expected annual loss, three hazard sub-scores, a disaster declaration count, and two CAL FIRE acreage percentages. Each cites an official programme, but none of those numbers appeared on the cited page during this audit, and the risk-index landing page carries no county values at all. Action taken: the values are withheld, the rows are blanked with the reported numbers disclosed in the notes, and the USGS earthquake probabilities — which were confirmed verbatim — are published as verified.

F-08 · Two official SCAG documents disagree about the region's future

SCAG's adopted 2024 forecast and its later draft materials differ in direction for population while agreeing on county employment growth. Only the adopted figures are published here, and they are labelled as assumptions-based planning scenarios rather than site forecasts.

Privacy and scope

What this audit did not do

  • No parcel work was performed. No address, parcel number, or ownership record was supplied, so no assessor, zoning, hazard, school, or flood lookup was run. Publishing the output of a lookup for a guessed parcel would be the most serious error available here.
  • No valuation was produced. No appraisal, comparable set, rent roll, or capitalisation rate is published; a cap rate has no official government publisher, so it appears only as a flagged sensitivity, never as a fact.
  • No personal information was collected, stored, or published. The audit searched every published page for email patterns and telephone patterns and found none; the brief requests no owner, tax, or financial data from the reader.
  • No source outside the official register was used to fill a value. Secondary commentary, broker material, and aggregators were not accepted as evidence at any point.
Privacy-first: no personal information is collected, embedded, or published. Property identifiers are intentionally omitted.
