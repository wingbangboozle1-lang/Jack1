Why a central-LA JIB, specifically

  • Format fit. JIB is a drive-thru-first QSR — the most resilient format through COVID (17.9% MSA unemployment trough, May 2020) and through office-distress cycles, because drive-thru captures car traffic independent of daytime office occupancy.
  • Customer base. LA city is 47.2% Hispanic/Latino, 35.7% foreign-born, with $21,281 retail sales per capita and $17.37B in accommodation & food services sales (2022) — squarely the JIB demographic and price point.
  • Brand concentration. JIB is heavily California/Texas weighted (its founding market), so central-LA JIB stores are typically long-established (the Westwood comp cited a 55-year operating history).
  • But: the same concentration means JACK's system-wide −4.2% same-store sales (FY2025) lands directly on this store's covenant strength.

What must happen before money moves

  1. Executed lease + all amendments + estoppel certificate (current NOI, expiry, options, guarantee type).
  2. Title report, ALTA survey, zoning letter, certificate of occupancy, ADA/seismic check, property-condition report.
  3. Tenant financial review: franchisee guarantor financials or JACK corporate guarantee verification; unit-level P&L if franchised.
  4. Parcel-level hazard checks: CAL FIRE FHSZ, CGS Alquist-Priolo, FEMA flood.
  5. BOV/appraisal + broker opinion on today's cap rate (no official cap-rate index exists — see Flagged for Review).
  6. CPA analysis of Measure ULA + 1031 mechanics before any listing; counsel on sale-document privacy.
