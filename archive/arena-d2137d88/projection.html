Illustrative projection — sell vs. renew vs. ride

A transparent, editable model. Defaults use flagged market norms (broker JIB average NOI $129,846; 10%/5-yr bumps). Every output is illustrative arithmetic — not an appraisal, not tax/investment/legal advice.

Inputs (edit freely — everything recalculates)

Assumes NOI bumps at the escalation rate each 5-year anniversary; ULA at 4%/5.5% on the full consideration (LA City conveyances); base 0.56% city+county transfer tax always applies; 1031 defers capital gains but not transfer taxes. Not modeled: property tax reassessment effect on tenant, vacancy downtime, tenant default probability, earthquake/fire losses, financing.

How to read this

  • “Sell now” = NOI ÷ sell-now cap, minus transfer taxes and selling costs. Simple, certain, one-time.
  • “Renew → hold → sell in 10 yrs” = present value of bumped rents over the new term plus exit value (bumped NOI ÷ renewal cap), minus taxes and costs, discounted at your required return. This is the C-option quantified.
  • “Ride to expiry, then sell dark” = PV of rents to today's expiry + sale at expiry at a harsher (residential-conversion/speculator) cap rate. Almost always the loser.
  • Whatever the model says, kill-switches from the Decision page override it: tenant distress beats any spreadsheet.
