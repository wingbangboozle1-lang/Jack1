What this means for the asset

  • Flat-to-shrinking city population (−0.8% since 2020) with the county down to 9.81M means the "growth story" is not population — it is traffic density, replenishment (35.7% foreign-born, 2.61-person households), and 2026–2028 event catalysts. Underwrite zero population growth; treat LA28 upside as option value, not certainty.
  • City median household income $81,939 (below the $90.8K county figure) and 16.5% poverty mean price-sensitive demand — an advantage for JIB's value positioning, a ceiling on any future rent the site can support at renewal.
  • 36% owner-occupancy and $1,933 median rent signal a renter, car-oriented, drive-thru-friendly trade area — consistent with central LA's corridors.
  • $17.37B restaurant spending (2022) is the relevant revenue pool; JIB's share is set at the store level, which is exactly why store-level economics — not county averages — decide the lease renewal.
  • Commute 30.7 minutes means much of central LA's daytime demand is commuter pass-through — stabilized by office/retail traffic rather than residents alone; this is why DTLA office distress (see Economy) matters despite the residential strength.
Schools are analyzed separately — see School Systems. For a NNN retail asset they matter mainly through neighborhood demographics and tenant foot traffic, not through district finance.
