2 · What “NNN” means for the Owner’s risk stack

Taxes → tenant

Prop 13 base-year value with ≤2%/yr cap; on sale, reassessment hits the buyer, not the Owner. Annual increases arepassed through to tenant under NNN — tax escalation risk is contractually transferred. Verify current tax bill & any direct assessments.

LA County Assessor — Prop 13

Insurance → tenant

Tenant carries liability + property coverage (typical NNN). Owner should still carry (i) loss-of-rents, (ii) earthquake (CA policies exclude it by default), (iii) landlord liability. Post-2025 wildfire/quake market: verify coverage limits annually; CA FAIR Plan ≈ $6B exposure in Palisades zone (Jan 2025) signals tightening availability.

FEMA — Jan 2025 wildfires

Maintenance → tenant

Roof, structure, parking lot, grease interceptors — typically tenant. Residual risks: (i) deferred maintenance at expiry/closure (dark NNN assets get cannibalized), (ii) compliance capex (ADA, seismic URM/soft-story retrofits) may default to landlord on re-lease, (iii) roof/parking life.

Verify with lease §maintenance + building report before any sale.
