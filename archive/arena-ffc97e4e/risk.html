Residual-risk logic
NNN shifts operating risk (taxes, insurance, maintenance) to the tenant, but cannot shift: (i) asset value/cap-rate risk, (ii) vacancy re-leasing risk, (iii) casualty destruction risk, (iv) tenant credit risk at the franchisee layer. Those four stay with the Owner until closing — which is precisely why the decision is “sell vs. renew,” not “hold forever.”

Doing neither — drifting toward 2030–2035 unrenewed — is the actual risk decision.
