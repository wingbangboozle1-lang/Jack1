Executive Summary

Jack in the Box, central Los Angeles —
sell before the 2030–2035 lease expiry, or renew?

A decision brief for a single anonymized, single-tenant, absolute-NNN quick-service restaurant in central Los Angeles, with a corporate/franchise NNN lease expiring in the 2030–2035 window. Every figure below is tied to a public source or explicitly flagged. No personal information appears on this site or in this research.

Asset type: Single-tenant QSR (Jack in the Box) Structure: Absolute NNN Lease expiry window: 2030–2035 Location: Central Los Angeles, CA Address & owner: withheld (privacy)
BOTTOM LINE (see Decision page for the full analysis): Do not ride the lease to expiry unrenewed. Start a parallel track now — (1) negotiate a renewal/extension with the tenant (target ≥10 years, 5–10% bumps every 5 years), and (2) prepare a sale process ready to launch if renewal terms fail or the tenant shows distress. If a ≥7–9-year term reset is signed: hold (maximum NNN value, optionality preserved). If not signed — or tenant credit deteriorates further: sell in 2026–2028 while the remaining term still supports NNN pricing, ideally via a 1031 exchange into a diversified replacement. Selling into a <5-year-remaining window is the worst outcome and should be avoided.

The decision at a glance

Why this decision matters now

1 · Tenant credit is the pivot

Jack in the Box Inc. (NASDAQ: JACK) reported FY2025 (ended 2025-09-28) revenue ≈ $1.5B, adjusted EBITDA ≈ $270.9M, system same-store sales of −4.2%, a discontinued dividend, a ~$115M Del Taco divestiture and a contested 2026 shareholder vote. It is not S&P/Moody's rated (broker-reported). 93% of the 2,136 restaurants are franchised — the store-level covenant is usually a franchisee LLC, not the JACK parent. (NNN / QSR market · sources: SEC EDGAR 10-K; filing summaries — see Flagged for Review.)

2 · Value decays as expiry approaches

NNN pricing is a function of remaining term × escalations × cap rate. With expiry in 2030–2035, every year of unrenewed runway widens the cap rate buyers demand. A reset 10-year term is worth materially more than the same asset at expiry minus two years.

3 · LA City sales friction is real money

Measure ULA adds 4% above ~$5.4M and 5.5% above ~$10.9M (Chained-CPI-adjusted; 2026 figures per LA City CAO), on top of ≈0.56% base city+county transfer tax. On a $6.5M exit that is ≈ $295K+ before capital gains — material to net proceeds. (ULA details.)

4 · Central LA fundamentals are mixed but stabilizing

LA city population 3.87M (−0.8% since 2020); median HH income $81,939; poverty 16.5%; LA County GDP $1.003T (current $, 2024); MSA unemployment 5.3% (2025 avg); multifamily occupancy ≈91% downtown; office vacancy elevated (22–33.3%, conflicting secondary sources); LA28 Olympics (Jul 2028) and D Line extension are real catalysts. (Economy, Demographics.)
