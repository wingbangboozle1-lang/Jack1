What NNN Means in Central LA Economics (Official Anchors)

Use Census/BEA to sanity-check rent sustainability:

  • Median HH income $81,939 (city) supports $1,933 median rent — rent burden is high, but foot traffic is high too. Your tenant’s sales must clear roughly 8–12% rent-to-sales to be healthy — verify against your actual sales (not published here).
  • Housing: 36% owner-occupied city vs 46.1% county (ACS) — renter-heavy core means less home-cooking, more QSR capture.
  • Travel time 30.7 min (Census) — car-centric commuters are drive-thru customers.
  • County GDP $818.5B (BEA) — even in population decline, economic mass retains food-away-from-home spend. 2022 retail sales per capita $21,281 (city, Census).
