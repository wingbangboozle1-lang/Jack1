## Note on cap rates (important honesty constraint)
No U.S. federal or California state agency publishes quick-service-restaurant (QSR) net-lease cap rates. Cap-rate inputs on this site are therefore **model assumptions**, flagged as such, never presented as official data. See U-06 on the Flagged for Review page.
