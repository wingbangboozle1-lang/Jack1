How to use this page

What area data can—and cannot—do

Can inform

Macro downside and upside cases

Use the official population, labor, and planning series to build cautious regional scenarios and stress-test a hold period. Keep geography and forecast date attached to each assumption.

Cannot establish

Restaurant unit economics

City sales and employment do not establish a specific unit’s sales, traffic, rent coverage, operator, customer profile, or renewal capacity.

Needs next

Parcel-matched evidence

A verified parcel, current demographics at appropriate geographies, site access, permitted use, and tenant unit data are required. Missing fields are listed on Flagged for Review.
