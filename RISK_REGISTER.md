# Risk register — current scope (2026-11-11)

The [published risk register](https://wingbangboozle1-lang.github.io/Jack1/risks.html) is generated from `data/verified-2026-11-10.json`, with linked official evidence, applicability limits and suggested mitigations. [Flagged for Review](https://wingbangboozle1-lang.github.io/Jack1/flagged.html) lists the parcel-level gaps. [Executive summary](EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md) gives the decision gates.

**Ordinary:** uncertain lease term/options, counterparty credit, rent and capex, loan/refinancing and transfer taxes, job/income changes. **Low-frequency/high-impact:** wildfire, earthquake, flood, casualty/insurance-market interruption and concurrent credit or rate shock. [FEMA county risk](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/products-tools/national-risk-index) and the [USGS hypothetical ShakeOut scenario](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/shakeout-scenario-a-hypothetical-mw78-earthquake-southern-san-andreas-fault) are regional context, not parcel hazard scores or probabilities. Obtain private parcel-specific hazard, coverage, condition, re-tenanting and liquidity evidence before pricing these risks.

Earlier text in this file assigned likelihoods and remedy deadlines and used assumed property economics without a verified parcel or lease; it is preserved in Git history **only**, not in the current public dashboard. No personal or property identifiers should be added here.
