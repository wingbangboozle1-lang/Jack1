Scope control · traceability · completion status

Every requirement, its status, and where to check it.

The brief asked for a defined set of deliverables. This page lists each one, states honestly whether it is met, partly met, or blocked by a missing property document, and links the page or official source where the work sits. Nothing is marked met unless it can be inspected.

How to read the status column

Met the deliverable exists and can be inspected on this site. Partly met the published part is sourced, but a stated dependency is missing. Blocked the requirement cannot be completed from public data and is listed on Flagged for Review with the document that would close it.

Why three requirements are only partly met

The dependency is a document, not an effort gap

R-04 · a dollar verdict

Needs the executed lease

Sell-versus-renew is a comparison of net sale proceeds against discounted ownership cash flows. Both sides require rent, term, options, obligations, and payoff. The decision page therefore ranks actions and states triggers, rather than printing a number it cannot support.

R-06 / R-08 · parcel factors

Needs a parcel match

School assignment, zoning, overlays, traffic, and hazard zones are all functions of an exact location. Substituting a citywide average would breach the no-inference rule, so the cells stay empty and the official lookup is linked.

R-14 · owner-specific optimum

Needs private financials

Basis, depreciation, debt, liquidity needs, and concentration determine the individual optimum. The brief deliberately does not collect them; the decision page shows how each one changes the ranking.
