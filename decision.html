Jack in the Box NNN decision brief — central Los Angeles area

Decision paths and balanced recommendation

A conditional, wealth-preservation-first course among selling, renewing, holding, exchanging, refinancing, and re-tenanting.

Provisional recommendation

Dual-track now; choose only after evidence.

Prepare sale and renewal cases in parallel. Preserve notice and option rights, verify the actual obligor and lease, and compare after-tax net sale proceeds (accounting for LA's 0.56%/4.56%/6.06% transfer-tax tiers) with risk-adjusted ownership value while monitoring Jack in the Box's 2029–2032 debt ARD wall ($1.430B / 95.8% of principal). Do not make an irreversible sell/extend choice or drift to expiry without a funded re-tenanting alternative.

Execution sequence

Next steps that do not require guessing

  1. Preserve rights: calendar notices/options and request estoppel/lease amendments review.
  2. Build the evidence file: title, parcel, lease, guaranty, rent roll, condition, insurance, tax basis, debt.
  3. Run two market processes: executable sale pricing and tenant extension terms.
  4. Compare after-tax net sale proceeds with risk-adjusted hold/renew/re-tenant value using verified inputs.
  5. If evidence remains incomplete, default to optionality and funded fallback—not an irreversible choice.
