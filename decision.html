Analysis · Decision

Sell before expiry, or renew?

Five options, one recommendation, explicit kill-switches. Built for your constraint set: preserve wealth, keep growth optionality, and keep risk manageable — with business-as-usual and black-swan cases both priced in.

RECOMMENDATION — “Renew or exit, never drift”: Run a 90-day parallel process starting now: (1) offer the tenant a market renewal/extension (target: 10 years, 5–10% bumps each 5 years, current-or-better rent, any combination of JACK parent guarantee + franchisee PG + security deposit/LC sized to 12 months), and (2) in the same 90 days, commission a broker BOV/appraisal and pre-negotiate exit alternatives (list, 1031, or sale-leaseback with a credit tenant). Signed renewal by Day 120 → hold (then re-evaluate sale in 2027–2028 with a full term). No signed renewal by Day 120 → go to market in 2026–2027 while ≥7–8 years of term remain. Never list with <5 years to expiry; never hold an unrenewed lease into the window.

The five options

Preferred path — Option C

C. Renew / extend first, then hold (or sell into a fresh term)

  • Target: 10-yr extension, 5–10% bumps / 5 yrs, NOI preserved-or-raised, PG from the strongest available obligor.
  • If signed: value re-rates up immediately (buyers pay for term); you keep optionality — sell in 2027–2029 with a full-term story, or hold for income.
  • If not signed: revealed preference — the tenant itself is telling you the store is marginal. Go to Option A immediately.

Risk: Low–moderate · Wealth preservation: High · Growth: Moderate (rent bumps + LA28/D Line upside)

Fallback — Option A

A. Sell now (2026–2027), pre-expiry

  • Capture remaining-term NNN pricing before decay; transfer ULA/Prop-13 friction once; exit concentration.
  • Typical discount vs a renewed-asset sale; tax friction ≈4–5.5%+ LA City transfer tax + capital gains (or 1031 deferral — ULA still applies).
  • Best when: renewal fails, tenant credit worsens, or 10Y keeps rising.

Risk: Moderate (execution + tax) · Preservation: Moderate–high · Growth: Depends on redeployment (1031 → diversified QSR/NNN portfolio elsewhere)

Hold but hedge — Option E

E. 1031 exchange / recapitalize on sale

  • Sell and defer gains into diversified NNN replacement (e.g., 2–3 investment-grade-tenant assets, different metros/industries) — kills single-tenant + LA-market concentration in one move.
  • Costs: exchange fees, tight ID/other deadlines (45/180 days), ULA still due on the LA City sale, replacement-quality risk.
  • Best when: a good exit price exists and suitable replacement inventory is available.

Risk: Moderate (execution) · Preservation: High if replacement quality is real · Growth: High (diversification premium)

Avoid — Option B

B. Hold, no renewal signed, ride to 2030–2035

  • Maximum value decay; buyer pool shrinks to speculators; last 2–3 years are nearly unfinanceable for buyers.
  • You absorb 100% of tenant-default, rate and black-swan (fire/quake/pandemic) risk with zero term premium.
  • Mathematically the worst risk-adjusted path unless renewal is already certain — in which case it's Option C, not B.

Risk: High · Preservation: Low · Growth: Negative (compound decay)

Ambitious — Option D

D. Re-tenant / redevelop at expiry

  • Box is trivially re-tenantable physically (any QSR fits), but you'd carry vacancy, TI, leasing downtime (12–24 mo), and lose NNN passivity.
  • Redevelopment (e.g., pad + drive-thru strip or TOC multifamily per LA Planning; the Westwood comp's C2-1VL-O TOC Tier 3 zoning hints at this class of play) is a developer's game — different risk class, needs capital, risk tolerance and entitlement appetite.
  • Only viable if parcel zoning is permissive AND basis is low. Verify zoning, TOC tier, and fault-line setbacks before dreaming about it.

Risk: High (execution, entitlement, carry) · Preservation: Low–moderate · Growth: Potentially highest — but not NNN investing

Why this balances preservation and growth

Wealth preservation is served by Option C (term premium, no forced sale at the bottom, ULA deferred), Option B never being allowed to happen, and Options A/E as ready off-ramps. Growth is served by keeping the asset option-rich: rent bumps compound, LA28/D Line upside accrues to the holder, and a reset-term sale in 2027–2028 harvests the catalyst. Risk is kept manageable by hard triggers, independent verification (BOV + appraisal + CPA + counsel) and by refusing binary exposure to one franchisee's solvency. This is a balanced posture: upside retained, tail risks transferred or hedged, no heroics required.

Explicitly out of scope / not advice: exact price, tax outcome, or legal conclusion. Those require the lease, the title, the parcel, the financials and your CPA/attorney. This framework is decision support, not a recommendation to consummate any specific transaction.
