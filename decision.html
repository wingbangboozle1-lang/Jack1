Wealth preservation with a growth option

Holding with a signed long-term renewal keeps income and preserves land optionality in a supply-constrained city; selling removes single-tenant and catastrophe concentration but crystallizes a shortening-term discount and a transfer-tax cliff. The balanced course is the dual track: secure the term, then decide with evidence rather than hope.
