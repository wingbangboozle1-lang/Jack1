Current conclusion: keep both paths open.
Confirm and preserve any existing contractual options and notice rights while preparing an independently supported sale case and a documented renewal alternative. A definitive sell/renew choice cannot be supported until the asset, lease, and owner-specific after-tax numbers are known.

Three strategies to model

Different paths protect different kinds of wealth

These are analytical alternatives, not recommendations. Each depends on the executed lease, parcel-specific underwriting, and private owner constraints.

Path A · Sell with tenant in place

Convert a leased asset to liquid capital

Potential preservation benefit: crystallize value, reduce single-property and tenant exposure, and create liquidity for diversified reinvestment.

Tradeoffs to quantify: price, debt discharge, broker/legal costs, transfer tax if applicable, income / capital-gain tax, reinvestment risk, and loss of future rent and optionality.

LA City ULA rules

Path B · Renew / extend

Retain income with updated contract protection

Potential preservation benefit: continue a contractual income stream and defer a sale decision, if renewal economics and obligor support are acceptable.

Tradeoffs to quantify: option language, renewal rent, term, guaranty, repair obligations, insurance, inflation exposure, tenant concentration, and capital retained in one asset.

SEC company lease disclosures

Path C · Hold to expiry / re-tenant

Keep the land and accept transition risk

Potential preservation benefit: maintain ownership and future-use flexibility if the site has durable demand and feasible alternate tenancy.

Tradeoffs to quantify: vacancy, downtime, leasing commissions, tenant improvements, capex, legal use, site access, hazard / insurance cost, and terminal value.

Transparent calculations

Compare on the same after-tax, present-value basis

Sale: cash actually available

Estimate gross price using a current independent opinion and bids, then deduct verified obligations and costs. Treatment depends on title, jurisdiction, transaction date, and owner tax records.

Net sale proceeds = price − debt payoff − closing / brokerage costs − transfer taxes − owner-level taxes

If LA City Measure ULA applies, use the rates and thresholds in force at closing—not a threshold projected from today. The actual jurisdiction and future sale date remain unconfirmed.

Official LA City ULA rules

Renew / hold: present value of ownership

Use the actual rent schedule and deduct all landlord-paid costs, taxes, insurance, capex, leasing costs, and vacancy exposure. Discount cash flows consistently and show terminal-value sensitivities rather than a single unsupported cap rate.

Hold value = Σ (net property cash flowₜ ÷ (1 + discount rate)ᵗ) + terminal value ÷ (1 + discount rate)ⁿ

Every item in the formula is currently unverified for the subject property. Do not plug in citywide averages or the public company’s consolidated financial statements.
