4 · Wealth preservation & growth balance

Preservation (capital safety)

  • Sell transfers: casualty tail (Puente Hills), insurance-failure tail, tenant-credit tail, re-pricing tail.
  • 1031 preserves capital; no CA/federal gain leakage on roll.
  • Concentration risk: one single-tenant asset → DST/portfolio diversification across geographies & credits.
  • Prop 13: buyer resets basis; Seller preserves low tax basis only if held — a cost of selling, priced into negotiation, not a reason to hold forever.

Growth (compounding)

  • 1031 into expanding market (Sun Belt NNN / necessity retail) captures appreciation this LA asset cannot (population −0.8%).
  • Proceeds can be split: partial 10X leverage into IG NNN + DST diversification; or UPREIT into REIT units (liquidity + income).
  • If renewing: demand ≥ market reset rent, ≥2% bumps, 10+ yrs — convert a wasting option into a bond-like asset; then re-evaluate sale at each anniversary.
  • Opportunity cost of passively holding a deteriorating single-credit asset through 2030–35: material, uncompensated.
Manageable-risk envelope
“Manageable” = (i) single-asset concentration eliminated or halved; (ii) tenant credit ≥ investment-grade or adequately collateralized; (iii) insurance incl. earthquake in force; (iv) ≥1 documented exit alternative (sale listing or DST backup ID); (v) ≤20% of net worth in one QSR NNN. The sell-now-1031 path satisfies all five; passive renewal satisfies only (iv) and (v).
