## The answer

**Prepare sale and renewal in parallel. Do not drift to expiry.**

Preserve notice and option rights, verify the actual obligor and lease, and compare
after-tax net sale proceeds with risk-adjusted ownership value. Do not make an irreversible
sell/extend choice — or drift to expiry without a funded re-tenanting alternative.

## Working premise (not a verified asset)

Reported Jack in the Box NNN-leased property near central Los Angeles with approximate

## Why this recommendation

1. **The tenant is under pressure, so renewal is a negotiation, not an assumption.**
   Q3 FY2026 (12 weeks ended July 5, 2026): system same-store sales **−1.1%** (franchise
   −1.2%, company −0.9%); 4 openings vs 17 closures; FY2026 guidance of ~2,100 units with
   50–60 closures, mostly franchise. Source: SEC Q3 FY2026 earnings release.
2. **Official population paths diverge.** California DOF (Baseline 2024 / Vintage 2026)
   projects Los Angeles County population **declining** (9,811,358 in 2026 → 9,390,823 in
   2050); SCAG Connect SoCal 2024 projects it **growing** (10,046,000 in 2019 → 10,793,000
   in 2050). The brief keeps them separate and never blends or assigns either to the parcel.
3. **The area is full, but not growing fast.** September 2026 unemployment: city/county
   **4.5%** NSA, metro 4.4%; metro payrolls +0.5% year over year. City population −0.8%
   since the 2020 base; county −3.2%. Median household income: city $81,939, county
   $90,112 (2020–2024 ACS).
4. **County hazard context is Very High, but it is not a parcel loss estimate.**
   FEMA NRI composite 100 (Very High); earthquake 100, wildfire 99.936, inland flooding
   100. Parcel FHSZ, flood zone, fault/soil, and insurance remain unverified.
5. **Exit costs are dated and threshold-based.** Measure ULA: 4% above $5,400,000 and
   5.5% at/above $10,900,000 for closings after June 30, 2026. Proposition 13 caps assessed
   growth at 2% per year. California taxes capital gains as ordinary income. Section 1031
   requires identification within 45 days and receipt within 180 days (or the return due
   date including extensions).
6. **The wage floor is a ratchet.** Fast-food minimum $20.00/hr (AB 1228); state $16.90;
   City of Los Angeles $18.42 from July 1, 2026. A renewal request must be priced against
   these floors.

## What can be done without guessing

1. Calendar every lease notice, option, estoppel, and assignment deadline from the executed lease.
2. Identify the legal tenant and guarantor; obtain financials and default history.
3. Order title, parcel/hazard/zoning match, condition report, insurance review, and tax-basis workup.
4. Request executable sale pricing and extension terms; compare after-tax net proceeds with risk-adjusted hold value.
5. Fund a re-tenanting fallback before expiry; keep sale and renewal options open until evidence supports a choice.
