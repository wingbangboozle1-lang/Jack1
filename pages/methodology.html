Projection System — How we turn official data into a decision

Auditable, no black box. Every input links to an official source; every assumption is explicit and changeable.

Principles

  • Official-only core: Population, income, employment, GDP, hazards, schools, transit from .gov sources only. Market data (cap rates, rents, broker comps) is flagged and never drives the base case alone.
  • Line-by-line verification: Each table row carries its source link. If not verifiable, value left blank + flagged.
  • No hallucinations: Forecasts are direct citations of CA DOF and SCAG. We do not invent growth rates.
  • Privacy-first: No address, APN, owner, or tenant sales on the public site. Analysis aggregated to community plan area.
  • Scenario-based: Base / Bull / Bear with triggers you can monitor.

7-Factor Weighted Framework

We score each factor 1–5 (1 = drag, 5 = tailwind) and weight by relevance to QSR NNN value in Central LA. Weights sum to 100. You can re-weight in the Decision calculator.

Valuation Guardrails (not a formal appraisal)

This brief is not a broker opinion of value. For a NNN, value ≈ NOI / cap rate, but both are market-sourced and flagged. We provide guardrails:

  • Prototype: Jack in the Box industry research lists avg sale $2,708,000, NOI $129,846, 2,500 SF, $1,083/SF, 0.75 ac, 20-yr term, 5–10% bumps/5yr — Market context — flagged.
  • Cap context: QSR 5.71% avg (Q2 2025), STNL 6.08% — QSR trades 37 bps tighter (premium for essential). Grocery anchors even tighter (Whole Foods 5.00%, Trader Joe’s 4.75%). Market — flagged.
  • Official anchor: We anchor to county GDP $818.5B and city income $81,939 to judge whether rents are sustainable in local economy — not to set price.

Any pricing decision requires a licensed appraiser and review of your actual rent roll and remaining term — intentionally not published here.

Verification Protocol

  • Line-by-line: Researcher checks each row against source URL, captures date accessed, and logs in Sources.
  • Flagging: If source is not .gov/.edu/official, row marked Unverified / Market and moved to Flagged for Review.
  • Parallel sessions: COORDINATION.md prevents duplicate edits; integration session re-verifies combined tables and checks no broken links before Pages deploy.
  • Reproducibility: All URLs are live and dated; FRED, Census QuickFacts, BEA, DOF, SCAG, USGS, CAL FIRE, FEMA NRI all versioned.

Limitations & Disclaimers

No legal, tax, or investment advice. Black swans (pandemics, major quake M≥7.0 in 30yr 46%, policy shifts) can dominate any forecast — see Risks → for FEMA EAL $4.57B quake / $2.81B flood and why NNN passthroughs may not fully protect you.
