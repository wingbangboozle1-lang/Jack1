Other Infrastructure Factors — Official Anchors

  • Roads/commute: Mean travel 30.7 min (Census) — car dependency sustains drive-thru. I-10/I-110 adjacency cuts two ways: visibility high, but freeway widenings (105, 5, 57/60 — Metro board 2025 agenda) cause multi-year detours.
  • Broadband: 92.6% households with broadband, 96.4% with computer (Census) — supports mobile-order QSR growth.
  • Water/drought: Los Angeles in episodic severe drought (Standardized Precipitation Index) — NOAA SPI — but retail EAL water factors low vs quake/flood.
