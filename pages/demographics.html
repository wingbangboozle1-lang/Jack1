Takeaway

Near-term (to 2030): City population is recovering (+31k in 2024) after pandemic losses; county still officially shrinking but slowly. Your QSR benefits from neighborhood density (Koreatown 101k people at 29k/mi²) more than county totals.

Long-term (2030–2045): Aging (+30% growth in 55+ per DOF slide) and lower births point to fewer K-12 enrollments (−17% LAUSD by 2030 projected) and softer fast-food frequency in family segments. International migration (avg 66,826/yr 2022–25) is the swing factor DOF says must rise to 107,010/yr to hold county flat.
