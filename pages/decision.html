Decision Framework — The three paths and when each wins

Balanced for preservation + growth at manageable risk. Use this to negotiate — not to replace counsel.

★
Recommended base case (reviewed against all official inputs): Dual-track — market the asset in the 2028–2031 window (18–30 mo before the 2030–35 expiry) while simultaneously negotiating a renewal. Whichever gives you a contract (sale or long renewal) first is the win. Do not hold to expiry without a signed renewal.

A. Sell Before Expiry

18–30 mo remaining • Buyer gets term

Upside

  • Locks in QSR investor demand ( avg 5.71% cap, premium to STNL) while term remains — strongest buyer pool when >1 yr term left
  • Transfers re-tenant + hazard repricing risk
  • Enables 1031 exchange into lower-hazard, investment-grade, or multi-tenant asset if you choose

Downside

  • Foregoes future appreciation if Central LA infill accelerates (SCAG +872k city pop capacity)
  • QSR cap expansion erodes price if rates rise
  • Transaction costs (broker, transfer, possible PI)

Best when

Renewal would be franchisee-only, short (≤5yr), flat rent, or tenant refuses refresh. Or insurance +29% Very High re-rating hits your NNN passthrough.

Risk if executed early: ★★☆☆☆ Low-MedModel →

B. Renew (Recommended if terms strong)

10yr+ • Corporate guarantee • Bumps

Upside

  • Converts expiry uncertainty into contracted yield — preserves “bond-like” NNN
  • Keeps foothold in densest trade area (8,304/mi² city; Koreatown 29k/mi²) + Metro growth tailwind
  • Escalations 5–10% /5yr (prototype) beat inflation in base case
  • Tenant funds refresh → extends box life 15–20 yr without your capex

Downside

  • Locks you into B+ credit longer; franchisor health (SEC shows liabilities > assets) matters
  • Hazard insurance may outpace escalations if Very High repricing severe
  • Opportunity cost vs selling near peak QSR demand

Best when

You get: (1) corporate or multi-unit financially strong guarantor, (2) ≥10yr, (3) bumps, (4) tenant refresh, (5) clear hazard/insurance split, (6) Seismic cert.

Risk if terms strong: ★★☆☆☆ Low-MedModel →

C. Hold to Expiry — No Renewal

Highest risk — not recommended

What happens

  • Buyer pool collapses <12 mo to expiry — only opportunistic bidders
  • Vacancy = you pay taxes, insurance, maintenance (NNN reverts)
  • Re-tenant requires seismic/ADA/fire code updates on your dime — scope unknown until lease ends
  • Black swan during vacancy is worst-case: no rent, full hazard cost, no leverage

Only consider if

You have a committed second tenant or redevelopment plan (e.g., Downtown Plan allows residential on commercial) and you can fund downtime. Otherwise, this is wealth-depleting.

Risk: ★★★★☆ HighAvoid

Tax & Wealth Notes (not CPA advice)

  • Sale may trigger depreciation recapture + capital gains — model net proceeds, not gross price. 1031 exchange defers if you reinvest in “like-kind” real property within 180 days (consult CPA).
  • NNN income is often favorable for estates (step-up basis) — holding for heirs can matter more than cap rate. Balance against hazard risk.
  • Entity matters: selling vs distributing entity interests changes transfer tax — verify deed type and proposition protections before market.

What Would Make Us Change This Recommendation

  • Toward Renew: LA City posts 3 consecutive annual population gains; QSR caps compress 50bps; tenant offers corporate guarantee + refresh.
  • Toward Sell Sooner: Insurance repricing +30% due to Very High re-rating; DOF decline steepens; tenant insists franchisee-only short renewal.
  • Monitor quarterly: BLS city unemployment (BLS ↗), Census PEP estimates (Census ↗), and CAL FIRE ordinance adoption status.
