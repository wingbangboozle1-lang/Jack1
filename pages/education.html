Forward Pressure — Enrollment Decline

!
DOF-driven: LAUSD enrollment mirrors county births. DOF projects 0–5 cohort falling, offset 5 years later in 5–17 cohort. LAUSD forecast: ~17% further decline by 2030. Fewer families = softer peak QSR demand on school corridors, but district remains countywide draw.

Higher Education — Regional Pipeline (official universities in LA)

  • UCLA (Westwood), USC (University Park), Cal State LA/Northridge, LACC trades — all within 5–12 miles of Central LA — supply professional workforce that lifts area incomes (see Economy → median HH $81,939).
  • In-migrants more educated than out-migrants — SCAG notes this selective migration keeps human capital high even as headcount falls.
  • For your QSR: student lunch/dinner trade area is real but smaller than family segment; 56.4% language-other-than-English homes (Census) signals multilingual staffing as competitive advantage.
