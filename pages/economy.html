GDP & Structural Economy

Scale: LA County $818.5B (current dollars) is the nation’s largest county economy >500k, essentially tied with Manhattan ($1.007T vs $1.003T). BEA also reports real GDP $802.0B in 2023, $790.0B in 2022 — growth but decelerating.

What it means for QSR: Massive healthcare, professional services, and retail base (201,972 prof/tech services jobs; 246,493 healthcare in city per Census Reporter) creates all-day traffic. But county GDP growth is slower than Sun Belt peers — SCAG notes region will add 3.7M people by 2045 vs 3.2M in earlier draft — slower than 2016–2045 baseline.

Per capita personal income LA County $83,888 (BEA 2024) vs NY County $217,075 — income concentration lower in LA, but absolute market huge.
