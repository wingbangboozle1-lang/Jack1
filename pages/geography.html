Geographic Takeaway for the NNN Investor

Tailwind: Central LA’s density and flat grid maximize drive-thru access and walkable lunch traffic — QSRs thrive on high vehicle + pedestrian counts. City’s Downtown Plan encourages mixed-use, more housing, and pedestrian-oriented commercial near transit.

Drag: Alluvial soils amplify seismic shaking (see USGS Professional Paper 1360 on Transverse Ranges faults). Flat, channelized LA River corridor raises riverine flood exposure = FEMA’s $2.81B EAL for riverine flood. WILDFIRE: CAL FIRE 2025 maps show Very High zones now include most of Silver Lake/Echo Park/Northeast LA — adjacent hills increase ember-cast risk even if your parcel is not mapped Very High.
