Geologic Detail — USGS (Official)

  • Faults: San Andreas (M8 capable), San Jacinto (M7), Puente Hills Thrust under downtown, Hollywood, Newport-Inglewood, Sierra Madre — 95 late-Quaternary faults capable in LA region (USGS Professional Paper 1360).
  • Shaking amplifiers: Alluvial soils + basin depth — same magnitude hits Central LA harder than bedrock sites. National Seismic Hazard Model (2024 update) increased shaking estimates for California. NBC/USGS ↗
  • What to verify: Year built, soft-story/URM status, retrofit ordinance compliance (LA has mandatory retrofit programs for wood-frame/URM).

Fire Detail — CAL FIRE (Mar 24, 2025)

  • New LRA maps classify moderate / high / very high for first time (2011 had only Very High). LA County Very High ↑29%; ~800k acres in 8 Southern CA counties in Very High; +1.4M acres newly subject to Chapter 7A wildfire building standards.
  • All of Malibu Very High; most of Silver Lake/Echo Park/Northeast LA added. Palos Verdes boundaries tightened. Central LA valley floor typically not Very High but adjacent hills drive ember risk.
  • County must adopt by ordinance within 120 days; not increasing zones beyond OSFM recommendation this cycle — but parcels may still see new requirements (defensible space 100 ft, vegetation management PRC 4291).
